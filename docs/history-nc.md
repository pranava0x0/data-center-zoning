# North Carolina: state air, municipal building and zoning

The pinned [PNNL inventory](https://github.com/IMMM-SFA/datacenter-atlas) assigns 26 physical markers to nine counties. Durham and Mecklenburg share the lead (five apiece); Rutherford and Wake have four each. Charlotte and Durham are tied among incorporated places with five apiece. A marker is not an operating facility, a permit, or MW.

The [Durham City-County inspections layer](https://webgis.durhamnc.gov/server/rest/services/PublicServices/Inspections/MapServer/12) and [Raleigh building permit layer](https://services.arcgis.com/v400IkDOw1ad7Yad/arcgis/rest/services/Building_Permits/FeatureServer/0) exposed 87 and 25 SQL candidate features. A stricter explicit sector match in project/description fields retained 83 and 23. Six loose-match exclusions are saved. Ancillary substation, chiller, demo and internal data-room work is included; these are project activities, not independent campuses. A CO status marks the underlying building permit as historical; it is not evidence a site ceased operations. Older records can be missing from these services. The Mecklenburg county building service denied access; the city's two official rezoning cases are an independent bounded source, not a county building census. Charlotte's withdrawn case was in its extraterritorial planning jurisdiction. Ranked Rutherford/Forest City requires further local-register work.

Seven numbered authorizations come from the [DEQ statewide active air permit register](https://www.deq.nc.gov/about/divisions/air-quality/air-quality-permitting): six named data processing facilities (SIC 7374) and the Hamlet-area Richmond County Duke permit. In [the joint Hamlet decision](https://www.deq.nc.gov/news/press-releases/2026/08/28/ncdeqs-division-air-quality-requires-additional-monitoring-testing-final-air-quality-permits-amazon), DEQ explicitly restricts Duke's temporary generators to powering Amazon's project. Amazon's on-site backup authorization is distinct. Sources in each row corroborate identity and role. Tapaha's T11 appears in the active register with a past expiry; continuing validity remains unconfirmed. Its prior T10 and modification application are separate events, not two more active instruments.

Run `python3 scripts/collectors/north_carolina/history.py` for offline replay (openpyxl); pass `--refresh` to refetch source bytes and rebuild. The manifest checks all downloaded sources by SHA-256. The Durham/Raleigh collector reconciles object-ID sets before normalizing; it does not export people or contact fields from the government originals into the published rows. Charlotte and Tapaha docket extracts are pinned separately, with primary links and field pointers. Source-provided dates/times use Eastern civil dates, except midnight UTC date-only fields which retain their displayed date. Duke and Amazon each receive the joint July 30 hearing notice, but it contributes no issued permit count.
