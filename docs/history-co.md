# Colorado permit and land-use histories

The scoped review through November 15, 2026 returns **46 dated events** in four counties. The [independent PNNL/Census ranking](../data/history/colorado/rank.json) contains 27 mixed facility markers: Arapahoe County 11, Douglas 6, El Paso 4, and Denver 3. Centennial leads incorporated places with seven, Aurora has four and Denver/Colorado Springs three each; seven markers are outside incorporated places. Denver leads the *recovered project records* because of the available city permit register. Neither number estimates statewide permits, built sites or MW. These markers differ from the broader 87-entry directory used for state scheduling.

[Centennial’s property search](https://www.centennialco.gov/Online-Services/Property-Search) and [address points](https://maps.centennialco.gov/arcgis/rest/services/Address_Points/FeatureServer/0) independently identify the street address on the Flexential DEN05 air application as Centennial. Several suites share one parcel, so no city permit is attributed by parcel alone. The [Centennial historical project-name layer](https://maps.centennialco.gov/arcgis/rest/services/History_EPL/MapServer/0) returned zero explicit or Flexential-name hits; that does not cover all city permit descriptions. Denver’s general narrative fields likewise returned zero direct candidates. Aurora’s government GIS service did not allow automated access; the public city document index provided independently verifiable records. These are explicit gaps, not absence findings. In counties without locality evidence the record stays at county level.

Run `python3 scripts/collectors/colorado/history.py` offline or `python3 scripts/collectors/colorado/history.py --refresh` for new snapshots. [Validation output](../data/history/colorado/validation.json) gives register denominators and place/county event rankings; [regression checks](../tests/test_co_history.py) cover state final-approval service, Aurora document dates, and Denver closure. Visually reviewed certificate hashes are pinned; a change fails replay pending reinspection. Construction and air issuances share neither identifiers nor status semantics.
