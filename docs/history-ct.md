# Connecticut: land-use, on-site power and a city permit

The five PNNL markers are grouped using the modern Census county equivalents (planning regions), not the abolished county governments. Western Connecticut leads with two markers. Norwalk and Stamford each have one in incorporated-place polygons. Two points outside the place layer are not necessarily outside a town: Connecticut towns are minor civil divisions. Hartford is included for its directly verifiable building register; no claim that it ranks first.

The [Hartford city register](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/building-permits-20200101-to-current) covers 2020 onward. We screened 11 broad candidates and retained one explicit description of work on a data center cooling tower. Other mentions of electrical data wiring or a center elsewhere in the description are not sufficient. Issued is preserved, with continuing validity unconfirmed if no date of expiry or explicit current term exists. The [Norwalk commission](https://www.norwalkct.gov/Archive.aspx?ADID=9949) documents two separate maintenance/surety decisions for the existing 10 Norden Place facility. A nearby boiler consent order was excluded: the same street address does not establish that an order governs the data-center portion of a multi-building development.

In [Trumbull Siting Council 1292](https://portal.ct.gov/CSC/3_Petitions/Petition-Nos-1291-1300/Petition-No-1292-Bloom-Trumbull), the Council imposed conditions and determined a certificate was unnecessary. The docket's 2021 completion notice lists two 1 MW rows commissioned on June 14 and 15, 2018. These are milestones on the 2 MW customer-side power facility. The local archive includes the filing, decision, and completion documents. Norwalk's portal supplied the municipal minutes through the publicly indexed version; two excerpts are pinned with source links.

Run `python3 scripts/collectors/connecticut/history.py` for offline replay, or use `--refresh` for new government originals. An unreviewed change to the scanned state ruling fails the digest assertion. Stamford’s individual permit cases are not covered; the statewide housing counts do not cover industrial permits.
