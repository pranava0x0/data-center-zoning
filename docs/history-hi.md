# Hawaii: City and County of Honolulu, and state environmental review

The PNNL point snapshot has no Hawaii markers. It supplies no county ranking here. Honolulu was chosen because the county publishes individual permits and the state has a named campus environmental-review file. This does not establish a statewide market-share ranking or that other counties lack facilities.

The [official city series, 2005 through June 2025](https://data.honolulu.gov/d/4vab-c87q) has five explicit textual matches in proposed use, job location or related fields. Four have different permit numbers and issuance dates; three report both completion and closed status. The fourth, 780719, is an ancillary school data center; it reports inspections in progress in the older series and cannot establish current validity. A Mapunapuna application was cancelled without an issuance or permit number and retains its application number. The old DPP fields do not supply a general free-text work narrative; projects represented only by street address or an unknown alias are missed. Current county records after the published end date are outside this scope.

A [state environmental notice, p. 3](https://files.hawaii.gov/dbedt/erp/The_Environmental_Notice/2011-02-08-TEN.pdf) publishes a Finding of No Significant Impact on the University of Hawaii IT center. The assessment identifies a full floor for the data center; the notice lists building and grading permits as necessary but does not establish their issuance. Uses and statuses remain separate. Run `python3 scripts/collectors/hawaii/history.py` for checksum-verified offline replay, or `--refresh` for new source bytes. Mayor/city jurisdiction here is the consolidated City and County of Honolulu: Kapolei and Ewa Beach are not separate municipal permitting authorities.
