## What was screened and excluded

- **Northern Virginia.** County search returned 1,245 fire rows on data-center parcels: 1,147 outside the selected fuel/hazard scope, 19 numbers already in the prior county feed, 36 lacking sufficient site identity, six repeated geometries. To qualify, the permit explicitly names the use or its complete street address and parcel match an earlier county data-center building record. [Loudoun Water pretreatment](https://www.loudounwater.org/commercial-customers/commercial-industrial-wastewater-program) and [reclaimed-water service](https://www.loudounwater.org/commercial-customers/reclaimed-water-program) do not identify a facility's issued permit. [Virginia DEQ's Birchwood notice](https://www.deq.virginia.gov/Home/Components/Calendar/Event/5266/139?toggle=next30days) confirms withdrawal of Amazon's GWI000502 reissuance request. Direct archival access was unavailable; targeted VPT searches for the number and name returned zero. This is a sourced lead, not an issued permit. The public-comment dates are not the withdrawal date.
- **Santa Clara/Bay Area.** Visually verified the city permit sheet itself. Its later submission in the **unrelated** Sequoia case was excluded according to the [later exhibit index](https://efiling.energy.ca.gov/Lists/ExhibitList.aspx?docketnumber=19-SPPE-03); this does not rule on the municipal permit. The original SC1 proceedings independently list [Appendix P](https://efiling.energy.ca.gov/GetDocument.aspx?tn=64063). [Lafayette project correspondence](https://efiling.energy.ca.gov/GetDocument.aspx?DocumentContentId=67672&tn=234818) does not establish a recycled-water authorization. Additional local records can be requested under [Santa Clara's fire-file procedure](https://www.santaclaraca.gov/our-city/departments-a-f/fire-department/divisions/community-risk-reduction-division/file-review-requests). SF: 18 permit-holder/DBA search results and two confirmed buildings, 365 Main St and 200 Paul Av (SFFD: 200–202 Paul Ave); only 12 approved storage/use records qualify.
- **Texas.** Dallas-area accessibility registrations are distinct from withdrawal/fire permits. The Coastal Bend Groundwater Conservation District's [May signed minutes](https://cbgcd.com/wp-content/uploads/pdf/Minutes-May-12-2026.pdf) approve two AWS drilling/operation permits, OP-26042003/004. A separate Amazon Data Services application was tabled and [remained pending in July](https://cbgcd.com/wp-content/uploads/pdf/Minutes-7-16-2026.pdf). The [hearing notice](https://cbgcd.com/wp-content/uploads/pdf/May-12-2026-CBGCD-Hearing-Notice.pdf) places wells on FM 442; the [state accessibility register](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/TABS/Search/Project/TABS2026023826) identifies Project Eagle in Boling. Neither proves these particular wells serve the campus or supply cooling. Keep them in the review queue. No Dallas/Fort Worth site-specific wastewater or water-right decision was confirmed here.
- **Columbus, OH.** Existing history includes [Ohio EPA surface-water permit to install DSWPTI1545394](https://edocpub.epa.ohio.gov/publicportal/ViewDocument.aspx?docid=2161104) for Amazon's CC-19612. Do not count twice. A PTI is not a continuing permit to withdraw or discharge. Two program URLs returned 404; the [industrial-pretreatment program guide](https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/epa.ohio.gov/Portals/35/pretreatment/IDPprogram.pdf) supplies context only.
- **Phoenix.** Query tank/hazard types on 19 municipal data-center project numbers. Of 12 candidates, four did not match the confirmed address and two explicitly named pharmaceutical tenants at the same redeveloped street address; all six are excluded. Project number and address cannot overrule a contradictory tenant name. [Phoenix's pretreatment annual report](https://www.phoenix.gov/content/dam/phoenix/waterservicessite/documents/2025_IPP_Annual_Report_Final.pdf) did not supply a sufficiently strong named-site match for another operator permit.
- **Pacific Northwest.** [EPA's Quincy case](https://www.epa.gov/waterreuse/water-reuse-case-study-quincy-washington) documents reuse, but the receiving works holds the regional reclaimed-water authorization. Do not attribute ST0005278 to Microsoft or Sabey. In Portland/Hillsboro, [Clean Water Services](https://cleanwaterservices.org/industry/data-centers-faqs/) explains how discharge conditions depend on the facility and its processes. No operator reclaimed-water right was confirmed in this sample.

## Replication

Run **python3 scripts/collectors/multistate/water_fire_authorizations.py** for offline extraction and **python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_water_fire_cases.py** for status, overlap and evidence checks. The optional --refresh flag and --only selector (loudoun, phoenix, sanfrancisco or documents) refresh a bounded source family. The default command does not access the network. Prerequisites are the checked-in Loudoun structure cache and previous Loudoun, Arizona and California municipal history outputs. Network acquisition respects host policy.

data/national/water_fire_sources.tsv lists primary sources; water_fire_cases.json contains curated anchored case facts; water_fire_review_queue.json and water_fire_visual_reviews.json identify exclusions and visually inspected evidence. cache/history/water-fire/source_index.json maps each source key to its URL, capture time and SHA-256, or the explicit access error. Immutable raw sources are under objects/. Every published record pins the source bytes. Local address filters, status vs facility status, utility permit exclusion, application/permit distinctions, and PA/WA permit keys are validated. This is a sample of specified registers and cases, not a census.
