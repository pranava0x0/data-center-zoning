# Louisiana: 24 sourced permitting and land-use events

The collection contains 15 LPDES water-coverage actions, three issued air decisions, one proposed air permit, one federal wetlands notice, and four local land-use actions. Fifteen federal NPDES version records duplicate a state number and issue date and are attached as evidence. The collector retains distinct dated actions on the same permit without counting each as a new instrument.

The [pinned PNNL Atlas](https://github.com/IMMM-SFA/datacenter-atlas/tree/fe4d8420f9fdc7b16b82b8461c5e4c39b93d8f1d) uses mixed building, campus and point markers; it does not measure statewide MW or unique projects. The place ranking intersects markers with [Census incorporated place boundaries](https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/Places_CouSub_ConCity_SubMCD/MapServer/11); permit localities instead follow the agency's facility location. A missing city remains a parish; Bossier's publishing-office address is not a project location.

**Collection.** [LDEQ quarterly air releases](https://www.deq.louisiana.gov/page/permits-issued-by-calendar-quarter) yielded three named initial permits after screening 11,501 rows in 37 distinct workbooks, Q1 2017 through Q3 2026. The official Q2 and Q3 2017 links both point to Q4 2017, so those quarters are not independently represented. [LPDES releases](https://www.deq.louisiana.gov/page/lpdes) yielded 15 matching actions from 3,638 lines with a recoverable permit ID across all 11 linked Q1 2024–Q3 2026 reports. State issuance, effective and expiration dates are separate. State/federal matching is by NPDES number and issue date; full [EPA source archive](https://echo.epa.gov/tools/data-downloads/icis-npdes-download-summary) has 39,284 Louisiana facility rows and 78,494 permit-version rows. This independently checks addresses and raw status. Where LDEQ and EPA disagree on an expiration date, present status stays unconfirmed. A general coverage number does not establish a final zoning or building approval. The [state notice](https://www.deq.louisiana.gov/public-notices?q=data+center) for Laidley 2460-00035-01 is a proposal; the initial 2460-00035-00 has a final decision on July 23, 2025. The state [PSC docket](https://lpscpubvalence.lpsc.louisiana.gov/portal/PSC/ViewFile?fileId=6PaAyqAncwA%3D) identifies the Evest data-center project; the [USACE notice](https://www.mvk.usace.army.mil/Portals/58/docs/regulatory/publicnotices/Public%20Notice%20MVK-2024-740_Final.pdf?ver=mi1h5xECRBUjFiqtHN9mqg%3D%3D) identifies Laidley's project, Section 404 application and related Section 401 request, not an approval.

Bossier [scheduled a March 18 hearing](https://www.bossierparishla.gov/docs/default-source/police-jury/meeting-documents/meeting-minutes/2026/3-3-2026-regular-meeting-minutes.pdf) and subsequently [approved the final plat](https://www.bossierparishla.gov/police-jury/news-events/news/2026/03/20/police-jury-awards-major-infrastructure-contracts--approves-final-plat-for-amazon-data-center-technology-park). The [Shreveport MPC denied](https://www.shreveportcaddompc.com/home/showpublisheddocument/17998/639004623366070000) special use/site-plan case 25-127-C December 3, 2025. The [December 18 city proceedings](https://louisianapublicnotice.com/notices/576942), municipal minutes republished in the public legal-notice archive, record adoption of the appeal by 7–0. Neither action is an issued building permit, and the denial is not the final outcome.

**Reproduction.** `python3 scripts/collectors/louisiana/history.py` replays quarterly spreadsheets, PDFs, public notices and local evidence; `python3 scripts/pipeline/place_rankings.py LA` replays IM3/Census. Run `python3 scripts/collectors/louisiana/deq_quarterly_reports.py --refresh` for the current air/water index/files; local decisions and rolling public-notice snapshots are pinned and require review when refreshed. Two sources blocking scripted PDF download have hash-pinned, indexed text extracts rather than original PDF bytes. Not all local permit systems, registrations, endorsements or statewide aliases were surveyed. The quarterly PDF input count is parseable permit-line coverage, not a certification of exhaustive text recognition. Q4 2026 is outside published quarter coverage at review time; absence in a ranked locality is not evidence that it has no permits.
