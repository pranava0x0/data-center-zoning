# Selected Plains, Mountain West and tribal rules

As of November 16, 2026, the original 19 reviewed provisions are retained in [the combined rule dataset](../data/national_rules_astra.json). [The source manifest](../data/national_rules_astra_sources.json) records public URLs and original bytes under `cache/ordinance-astra/`. Run `python3 scripts/ordinances/rules/national_rules.py` to hash-check every cited response and match each short evidence anchor on its stated page. `--fetch` fills missing originals; it does not change existing pins. For a new version, preserve the previous manifest first. Supplemental corroboration and adoption-date documents are included in the manifest; the indexed rules cite one main document each.

* [Bismarck's Land Development Code](https://www.bismarcknd.gov/DocumentCenter/View/55775/Land-Development-Code?bidId=), effective [October 1, 2026](https://www.bismarcknd.gov/2371/Zoning-and-Guidelines), distinguishes three load/capacity classes, explicit permit pathways, residential separation and written utility capacity. Use the official zoning map to determine city zoning jurisdiction and parcel district; a city place polygon is not the jurisdictional boundary in the extraterritorial area.
* [Missoula County](https://www.missoulacounty.gov/news/new-podcast-episode-what-can-missoula-county-do-about-data-centers) confirms that interim zoning covers new and expanded facilities outside city limits. Source is the county's adoption confirmation, not the text of a separate Missoula City ordinance.
* [McPherson County Ordinance 2025-1](https://mcpherson.sdcounty.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2025-1-Data-Center-Ordinance_signed.pdf) was effective August 5, 2025. The county [published a November 5, 2026 hearing](https://mcpherson.sdcounty.gov/notice-of-public-hearings-ordinance-2026-1/) on a proposed amendment. The [published ordinances register](https://mcpherson.sdcounty.gov/commission/county-ordinances/) supplied 2025-1, not an executed 2026-1. Therefore each row states its version and does not claim superseding terms or a resolved November vote.
* [Bannock County's ordinance](https://www.bannockcounty.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Data-Center-Moratorium_Final.pdf) explicitly protects already-issued permits and certain earlier applications. The county [explains its jurisdiction and end date](https://www.bannockcounty.gov/datacenter/). Its separate excavation/grading FAQ addresses the general permitting process; it does not dissolve the moratorium for data centers.
* Laramie County's [approved Switchgrass zoning action](https://laramiecounty.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?GUID=277FD852-B4BB-4AE6-8EA0-CF89F06D3B5F&ID=7691002&Options=&Search=) incorporates a PUD exhibit. Only those lots have the use permission recorded here. Laramie City proposals on the November 17 schedule are not treated as adopted.
* The [Cherokee Nation's government announcement](https://www.anadisgoi.com/index.php/government-stories/cherokee-nation-issues-data-center-task-force-report-new-policy-actions) supplies two executive policy facts, not two permitting cases. On non-tribally-owned lands within the reservation, the consultation rule conditions the Nation's support; it is not a reservation-wide land-use ban. The [tribal government](https://www.cherokee.gov/our-government/executive-branch/task-forces/data-center-task-force/) identifies Anadisgoi as its own newsroom. No county, state or statistical tribal-area GEOID substitutes for a land-title check. Secondary-source reports from other nations were not promoted in this supplement.

The [second pass](national-rules-astra-expansion.md) adds five state/local jurisdictions groups plus an additional tribal and territorial rule.
