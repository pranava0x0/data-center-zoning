# New York permit and planning histories

At the November 15, 2026 cutoff the replay publishes **465 source events** across seven counties, dated August 1990 to October 2026. Of those, 455 are City of New York records. This is a sample of discoverable work on explicitly identified data facilities/rooms (including smaller alterations), not a statewide permit total, facility count, or estimate of capacity.

The [PNNL/DOE atlas](https://github.com/IMMM-SFA/datacenter-atlas) and [Census incorporated-place layer](https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/Places_CouSub_ConCity_SubMCD/MapServer/11) produce 37 mapped facility markers, led by Niagara County (13), Rockland (4), and Albany and New York counties (3 each). Among incorporated places, Niagara Falls has 12 and New York City 6; 16 markers fall outside incorporated-place polygons, which in New York can still be governed by a town. The directory's statewide count and these mapped points have different inclusion rules. Likewise, Manhattan leads the actual source-event sample because of DOB coverage; it does not follow that Manhattan has the most facilities. See [rank data](../data/history/new_york/rank.json) and [coverage and query counts](../data/history/new_york/validation.json).

City searches use coarse literals (US/UK spellings, server farm, data processing center), then verify word boundaries and join identity. Untagged aliases, spelling variations, and standalone street addresses can be missed. State air evidence is project-specific, not statewide environmental permitting history. Some Niagara Falls archive PDFs refused retrieval; pinned official indexed excerpts and record-level council links are provided for the published votes. These are not an exhaustive docket or a final determination about every later city action. The gaps do not establish absence of rules or permits.

From the project directory run `python3 scripts/collectors/new_york/history.py` for offline replay, `python3 scripts/collectors/new_york/history.py --refresh` to update the public source registers, and `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_ny_history.py`. Local case evidence is pinned and separately reviewable in [case fixtures](../data/history/new_york/cases.json). The [collector](../scripts/collectors/new_york/history.py) stores hashes and row-level links; the [status mapping](../scripts/collectors/new_york/status.py) explicitly separates issuing registers from applications and review decisions. Rerun geographic priorities with `python3 scripts/pipeline/place_rankings.py NY`; use `--refresh` to refetch the pinned atlas and Census polygons.
