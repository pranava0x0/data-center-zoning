# Indiana: 458 recorded actions, 39 state design projects

This is a targeted project history through **November 15, 2026**, not a census of Indiana facilities or permits. The 458 rows comprise 369 numbered state design filings, 71 state design releases, 11 state air actions, two state waterways actions and five municipal planning votes. The first recorded action is November 9, 2007; the last is November 13, 2026. A facility can have dozens of filings for one building. In particular, 291 of the 369 filings are tagged **COR** in the register; counting every filing as a new permit would materially overstate construction.

## Where we searched first

The [PNNL IM3 atlas](https://im3.pnnl.gov/visualizations) supplies [observed, OSM-derived centroids](https://immm-sfa.github.io/datacenter-atlas/im3_datacenter_centroids.geojson), version 2026.11.02. Following Ohio, we used the reported state/county, then intersected the points with [2026 Census BAS incorporated-place boundaries](https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/Places_CouSub_ConCity_SubMCD/MapServer/11). The offline build verifies the complete polygon OBJECTID set against an independent IDS query. The result ranks **mapped records**, not unique operating sites or announced gigawatts.

## Evidence and units

* **State construction design.** The [Indiana DHS Project Activity service](https://www.in.gov/dhs/building-plan-review/project-activity-includes-real-time-plan-submissions-and-releases/) reports a project number, named county, project and facility use, links to forms, and links to releases. [DHS describes the purpose and possible results of plan review](https://www.in.gov/dhs/building-plan-review/building-plan-review-process). This is a state plan/design record, not a permit issued by a municipal building official. Example: SBC 450637 contains a [December 20, 2024 application](https://oas.dhs.in.gov/reports/rwservlet?dfbsepnpdf&report=plan_application_information.rdf&p_form_id=1140442) and a [January 8, 2025 standard release](https://oas.dhs.in.gov/reports/rwservlet?dfbsepnpdf&report=plan_design_release04292005.rdf&p_release_id=515445).
* **Search and cross-check.** Screen 35 monthly filed-date windows (Jan 2024 through Nov 15, 2026) in St. Joseph County with mailing city New Carlisle; supplement with site street-number + city + county queries in the config. The web form only filters a numerical street component; the register can return unrelated premises (schools, offices, restaurants). 114 distinct candidate SBC numbers; 35 matched explicit project name or use and four more had explicit use on the primary application. The other 75, including coded buildings, remain in the audit file. The broad Purdue query produced an Oracle error; narrower 2024–26 queries recovered the relevant file. A query that errors is not a negative search.
* **Count at the event level.** For all 39 accepted projects, requery without a date filter using the exact SBC number; check that the returned project number matches. Deduplicate duplicate browser links/months by form ID or release ID, not address, customer or year. Keep every numbered filing and release through the cutoff, even when an event lies outside its original discovery month. 369 filing codes: 291 COR, 39 STD, 18 PRT, 15 ADA, 6 FDN. 71 releases: 38 Standard, 17 Partial, 11 Addendum, 5 FDN. The report’s aggregate query can display related project activities outside the requested discovery dates. The exported date is the individual form filing or release date. A correction filing is not a separate new building, approval or completion.
* **Geography.** The state’s named county is copied from the actual project entry. Where available, the representative application’s “Is project in city limits?” answer and city are extracted from its Project Location block. Only an explicit Yes promotes the history to the named city. A No or missing response leaves a county row, and missing city fields are not inferred. IDEM-only entries are kept at stated county. City-issued Hobart votes are attributed to Hobart; their county field is deliberately blank because the cited vote text does not establish it.
* **Air and waters.** Five signed air decisions and six draft air files. Two distinct source and permit modification numbers share [one Amazon notice](https://www.in.gov/idem/files/notice_20261118_air_50362-50375pn-js.pdf). Comments opening dates come from the archived [IDEM all-region public notice table](https://www.in.gov/idem/public-notices/public-notices-all-regions/); a future comment deadline does not become a decision. Distinct source decisions at [55001 Larrison](https://permits.air.idem.in.gov/47750f.pdf) and [31100 SR 2](https://permits.air.idem.in.gov/48298f.pdf) are not merged by company. For wetlands, the [2025 deficiency/non-compliance letter](https://ecm.idem.in.gov/cs/idcplg?IdcService=GET_FILE&Rendition=web&dDocName=83838498&dID=83834455&noSaveAs=1) and the [2026 hearing notice on the resubmitted §401 application](https://www.in.gov/idem/files/notice_20260710_401_2025-78-71-enh-wqc-2.pdf) are neither approvals nor proof of present status.
* **Municipal land use.** The [Hobart April work-session agenda](https://www.cityofhobart.org/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_04072026-2552?MOBILE=ON) identified two distinct site-plan petitions. The [May 7 signed minutes](https://www.cityofhobart.org/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Minutes/_05072026-2563), PDF pp. 2, 11, 14, give the actual votes: 26-12 site-plan approval; 26-16 favorable recommendation on rezoning, not legislative adoption; 26-17 preliminary two-lot plat; 26-14 site-plan approval with third-party inspections and redesign of drainage/swales; 26-18 preliminary one-lot plat with a required 40-foot right of way. These are five actions on two related projects. Both site-plan votes were 8–0. The PDF is scanned; local OCR is cited by PDF page and locked to the archived PDF hash. Visual review confirmed the vote boxes and the explicit grant motion, not just the agenda checklists.

## Rebuild, review and extend

From the project root, run `python3 scripts/collectors/indiana/history.py` to rebuild the ranking, case history and coverage audit **without network access**. Only the existing pinned pypdf dependency and standard library are needed. `--fetch` resumes configured public query collection, cases and application captures. For a new vintage, copy the previous snapshot, revise the configured cutoff/coverage dates and reposting references for newly reviewed notices, and refresh sources deliberately. The live notice index rotates and release detail can change; exact historical captures must be kept for audit. The optional macOS helper `swift scripts/lib/ocr_vision.swift input.pdf 18` recreates line arrays from scanned municipal minutes. It is not needed for offline rebuild. Primary sources are gzip-compressed under their SHA-256; SHA applies to decompressed bytes. The manifest preserves the actual query URLs, bounds, errors and selected references. The records link to official form/release PDFs or decisions, not an unofficial project directory.

The Ohio geometry/source validation transfers; Ohio’s permit-table schema does not. Indiana required filing/issuance separation, numeric county selector codes with leading zeros, HTML report pagination and repeated headers, separate source numbers, and local boundary declarations. Remaining priorities: examine actual [South Bend/St. Joseph local building reports](https://southbendin.gov/department/community-investment/building/) separately from state releases; corroborate the [South Bend industrial wastewater docket](https://docs.southbendin.gov/WebLink/DocView.aspx?cr=1&dbid=0&id=389839&repo=sbend), whose listing identifies a permit application without proving issuance; obtain Columbus historical building and occupancy records; screen IDEM stormwater files and operator aliases for Hancock/Morgan. The state air decisions are document samples rather than an exhaustive [state permit-status register](https://caats.idem.in.gov/) export. City-level permits, wastewater certificates, and uncaptured years or jurisdictions must not be reported as zeros.
