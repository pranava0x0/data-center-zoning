County and municipal priorities use [PNNL mapped facility markers](../data/national/hotspots_audit.json), which cover fewer facilities and do not measure gigawatts. Ties are kept. A dash means no ranking source for this area. A zero means no published atlas record in that column, not that a jurisdiction has no law or permit.

The CSV also includes the leading statistical places, such as Ashburn, Virginia. They identify a concentration of facilities, not a separate permitting government. The local CSV counts rules tied to that Census identifier and history rows explicitly naming the city or county; missing geography can leave genuine cases unmatched. A county history count can include projects inside a city. Federal records are already included in history. Never sum the county and city columns, or equate a history row with a distinct permit.

[Local permit follow-up queue](../output/priority_local_backlog.csv): leading counties, cities and townships with no matched action in the [reviewed local registers](../output/local_register_examples.csv). A federal or state case in a county does not establish coverage of its local permit office. Unclassified registers are excluded from these lower bounds. A local-government record at a site in a county can be city-issued; see the register name and primary citation. The full locality CSV includes dates only through the edition cutoff and counts unique permit keys even when a representative row appears elsewhere.
