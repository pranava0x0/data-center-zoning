# Montana project records

Nine project actions cover a proposed water supply and road agreement in Yellowstone County, and a withdrawn land-use case in Missoula County. Yellowstone, Custer and Granite each have one mapped PNNL centroid; Billings, Philipsburg and unincorporated land have one apiece. This small observed inventory misses proposals: the Missoula case was pursued independently of the marker ranking. Active corresponding building permits were not established in these jurisdictions.

Missoula County's [Bonner project timeline](https://missoulacountyvoice.com/bonner-data-center/) records four dated incomplete revisions numbered 1, 2, 3 and 5. The county's later update says the property owner withdrew his signature and that the project will not go forward. It does not date the update. No revision 4 or denial vote was inferred. Montana's [signed habitat consultation](https://deq.mt.gov/files/Water/WQInfo/Documents/Subdivisions/Quantica%20Big%20Sky%20Campus/7514%20Signed%20Letter%2020260902.pdf) is separate from DEQ's [public water supply notice](https://deq.mt.gov/News/pressrelease-folder/Big-Sky-Campus-well-09-29-2026). Neither authorizes withdrawal or establishes a final water right. Yellowstone County's [October 27 road agreement packet, page 9](https://www.yellowstonecountymt.gov/BOCCDocs/2026/BOCCREG/20261027_1625/AGENDApacket__10-27-26_0219_1625.pdf#page=9) contains a staff recommendation. The county minutes archive did not supply the corresponding meeting minutes at retrieval, so the source alone does not establish the final vote.
