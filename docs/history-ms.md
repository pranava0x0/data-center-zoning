# Mississippi: 30 dated permitting and siting records

The sample contains 16 Mississippi DEQ register events, 12 additional EPA permit-version records and two Southaven land-use decisions. Seven same-permit, same-day state/federal matches are attached as corroborating evidence rather than counted twice. Distinct permit counts and underlying data centers are different measures.

|---|---|---|
| County | Madison 9; DeSoto 2 | Madison 13; DeSoto 8; Hinds and Warren 3 each; Lauderdale, Oktibbeha and Rankin 1 each |
| City | Ridgeland 4; Southaven 2; 5 outside incorporated places | Canton 9; Southaven 8; Ridgeland 4; Vicksburg 3; Clinton 2; Brandon, Jackson, Meridian and Starkville 1 each |

[PNNL IM3 release](https://github.com/IMMM-SFA/datacenter-atlas/tree/fe4d8420f9fdc7b16b82b8461c5e4c39b93d8f1d) markers may count multiple buildings in a campus. Source counties and [Census incorporated-place polygons](https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/Places_CouSub_ConCity_SubMCD/MapServer/11) supply this separate priority proxy. State permit towns are address labels, not assertions of municipal jurisdiction. Results do not rank MW, annual construction, or a statewide census of permits.

**Sources and decisions.** [MDEQ enSearch](https://opcgis.deq.state.ms.us/enSearchOnline/) covers air, water and other named programs, not all state programs or official administrative files. The in-scope AI and permit numbers are explicitly pinned in the script: repeat owners, unknown subsidiaries, stockpiles, existing generators and warehouse/logistics facilities are not assumed to be data centers. [Mississippi Development Authority](https://mississippi.org/news/amazon-continues-mississippi-expansion-announcing-plans-to-invest-a-total-of-25-billion-across-the-magnolia-state/) identifies the Madison and Clinton campuses; MDEQ's [JAN 220 construction certificate](https://opcgis.deq.state.ms.us/enOnline/get_doc.aspx?dt=pdf&id=1853771) identifies the Warren project as contractor work. The [MZX application and board meeting](https://www.mdeq.ms.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Signed-Minutes-of-the-March-2026-Permit-Board-Meeting.pdf) concern an associated power plant; the collector excludes former power-plant owners. The [Southaven board minutes](https://southaven.org/ArchiveCenter/ViewFile/Item/1813) are visibly source-checked on PDF pages 29–31: on September 1, 2026, the board approved the subdivision by seven votes and approved the design with conditions by six votes to one. Neither is a building permit.

The complete Mississippi slice of [EPA's ICIS-NPDES release](https://echo.epa.gov/tools/data-downloads/icis-npdes-download-summary) contains 9,495 facility rows and 17,487 permit versions. A stable NPDES ID identifies water coverage independently of operator/project names; different versions and enacted decisions carry separate evidence. Three MDEQ records list coverage under an active heading and also explicitly record same-date termination: [MSR109658](https://opcgis.deq.state.ms.us/ensearchonline/ai_info.aspx?ai=12592), [MSR109732](https://opcgis.deq.state.ms.us/ensearchonline/ai_info.aspx?ai=90111), [MSR109762](https://opc.deq.state.ms.us/ai_info.aspx?ai=90292). The EPA label on each same-date record is EFF. Current validity is **unconfirmed**. The publication of a pending modification, notice, or additional-information request does not invalidate a separately issued instrument.

**Replay and limitations.** Run `python3 scripts/collectors/mississippi/history.py` (offline), `python3 scripts/pipeline/place_rankings.py MS` (offline ranking), and `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_ms_la_status.py`. To refresh EPA, use `python3 scripts/history_ms_la_epa.py --zip cache/history/ms/epa-national.tmp.zip --download`; add `--refresh` for Census/PNNL ranking. The state source declined direct scripted requests; reviewed public renderings of permit tables are cached in `cache/history/ms/ensearch-*.txt`. These are normalized observations, **not original HTML**; each has a SHA-256 and an agency URL. Recheck additions, changed tables, and new dates against the state site before accepting a new snapshot. Local board documents are a targeted search; many building permits, water withdrawals, site approvals and unnamed developments will be absent. These results do not establish what rules apply at a particular address.
