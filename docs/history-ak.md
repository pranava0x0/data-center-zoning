# Alaska: municipal permits and project-specific land decisions

The pinned PNNL atlas has no Alaska markers. A state or municipal market-share ranking cannot be estimated from it. We selected Anchorage for its [municipal building records](https://bsd.muni.org/Inspandreview/PermitInfo.aspx?apbldgkey=355517) and the North Slope for two state-documented data center projects. An initial bounded selection of four public parcel registers and two explicit project cases produces 11 building records and 43 dated inspections or discipline reviews. The municipal permit pages do not give an issuance date: issued/closed status is preserved, and inspection/review dates belong only to those events. No number is inferred from an inspection date. A data conversion placeholder is excluded; a deleted file and a hospital change order remain case history, not separately confirmed permits. Several cases are data rooms within schools, offices or hospitals; they are not independent operating campuses.

The [state DNR’s final decision on STAK ADL 422741](https://aws.state.ak.us/OnlinePublicNotices/Notices/View.aspx?id=225835) offers a negotiated 50-year state land lease, conditional on subsequent steps. The five-year entry authorization form is not evidence that it was signed. The [Duck Island trial notice](https://aws.state.ak.us/OnlinePublicNotices/Notices/View.aspx?id=217467) seeks permission for a small bitcoin data center, but does not grant the land use permit. The [FERC Cook Inlet notice](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-09-04/pdf/2026-18151.pdf) accepts a tidal energy study application tied explicitly to subsea compute modules; it does not give construction rights. The state's general industry fact sheet is not included in permit history.

Run `python3 scripts/collectors/alaska/history.py` for checksummed offline replay; `--refresh` refreshes the six municipal seeds, agency case documents and matched permit case pages. Missing case dates remain null. The borough is recorded only when the municipal consolidated jurisdiction or state evidence supports it.
