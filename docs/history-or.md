# Oregon: permit documents and land-use decisions

The [DEQ permit program](https://www.oregon.gov/deq/aq/aqpermits/pages/default.aspx) transferred applications to [Your DEQ Online](https://ordeq-edms-public.govonlinesaas.com/pub/pub-rcd/home) in November 2024. This extraction covers **the older document register**, not the modern system. A label on that register cannot establish present-day authorization.

The pinned [PNNL inventory](https://github.com/IMMM-SFA/datacenter-atlas) has 134 Oregon markers. Morrow County has 38, Washington 36 and Umatilla 34; incorporated places are led by Hillsboro (33), Umatilla (20) and Prineville (11). Forty-three markers fall outside a 2024 Census place. One marker need not be one facility: a [state advisory committee summary](https://www.oregon.gov/energy/get-involved/Documents/7-31-2026-DCAC-Session-Meeting-Summary.pdf) uses a different geographic and facility grouping. The inventory and generalized boundaries are not an operating-capacity survey.

We walked every permit page for seven counties (Morrow, Washington, Umatilla, Crook, Wasco, Multnomah and Deschutes). Of 1,004 register rows, 37 matched an operator or sector string. We retained only individual permit files for facilities where the permit's text confirms a data center or the relevant hosting NAICS. We excluded three rows, including generic master permits. Distinct documents were downloaded once even if linked by multiple rows. The 34 retained permit versions produced 28 additional locally approved compatibility findings with a verified authority and date. The local finding is not a second air permit, and the state source number is not a local permit number. Twenty permits lack a verified exact signing date: the register year is recorded separately. The snapshot has no coverage claim for unnamed operators or remaining counties.

Three additional county/city records are curated: a numbered Morrow zoning permit, an airport-adjacent land-use decision and a Umatilla power-line/substation hearing. For [LUD-N-68-24](https://www.co.morrow.or.us/sites/default/files/fileattachments/planning_commission/meeting/packets/16790/09242024_pc_packet.pdf), the September packet includes draft August 27 minutes showing a 4–3 approval. The [Umatilla staff report](https://www.umatilla.gov/sites/default/files/fileattachments/planning_commission/meeting/6983/2-23-2021_planning_commission_packet-compressed.pdf) schedules a hearing; it does not document issuance. The locally pinned casebook contains primary web-index extracts and page references where original bytes were not available for direct archiving.

Run `python3 scripts/collectors/oregon/history.py` for an offline rebuild; `--refresh` snapshots register pages and originals and regenerates OCR on macOS using Vision. Replay requires PyMuPDF but does not require macOS. Byte hashes are validated before processing. Eleven image-only originals have source-hash-bound transcripts in the OCR file. Geography, held rows and source counts are written separately to data/history/oregon/*.
