# Wisconsin

Two official [DNR air tracking](https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/AirPermits/Search.html) facility searches, including the portal’s “Show Inactive Permits” checkbox, returned fourteen numbered records: twelve at [Microsoft Mount Pleasant](https://apps.dnr.wi.gov/warp_ext/AM_PermitTracking2.aspx?id=35961), two at [Vantage Port Washington](https://apps.dnr.wi.gov/warp_ext/AM_PermitTracking2.aspx?id=38402). There are 47 dated application, complete-application, public-notice and decision events. A register’s “Decision” date has to be paired with an explicit active or inactive record to be counted as a permit. Prior Fxx revisions share the facility operating permit family; a requested revision with no decision date does not supersede an issued instrument, and no decision is inferred from a blank or X. State staff’s [final approval](https://apps.dnr.wi.gov/warp_ext/AM_DownloadObject.aspx?id=1275107) supports the latest Mount Pleasant air decision. DNR groups multi-part actions: these are not data-center counts.

The [Lighthouse case](https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/EIA/Portwashington.html) yields ten further municipal-service, air-hearing, wetland, stormwater and environmental-review actions. The agency says it conveyed stormwater coverage, but the statewide master general permit number is not a site permit number; the combined statement is shown as an unnumbered action. Exemptions, assessments and a hearing are not issuance. The two DNR air issuances also described on the Lighthouse page are already present in the register and are not counted twice.

Three [Mount Pleasant meeting records](https://www.mtpleasantwi.gov/2180/Plan-Commission) describe commission-level master plan and site votes; in particular [SP-23-16](https://www.mtpleasantwi.gov/Archive.aspx?ADID=4017) is a recommendation, not Village Board or building permit approval. The indexed municipal minutes were reviewed; a direct request for historical archive PDFs returned 404, so the hash is over the extracted manifest rather than claimed original files. [Madison's official moratorium](https://www.cityofmadison.com/dpced/about/temporary-data-center-moratorium) is distinguished in the rule layer from its draft permanent replacement.

Run `python3 scripts/collectors/wisconsin/history.py` or `python3 scripts/collectors/wisconsin/history.py --refresh`. The latter uses the portal's public form and drops session values before caching records. As of the November 15, 2026 cutoff the ranking is 42 Racine County / Mount Pleasant, 18 Ozaukee County / Port Washington source events. Racine was the first target in the national marker ranking. Milwaukee, Dane and other Wisconsin counties are not represented in this project-event pilot; no inference of an absence of regulation or past permits is supported. Additional permit numbers and municipalities need search and validation.
