# New Mexico permit history

The [geographic ranking](../data/history/new_mexico/rank.json) puts Bernalillo first (9 mapped features), then Valencia (5), Doña Ana (3) and Santa Fe (1). Albuquerque leads incorporated places (7), followed by Los Lunas (5). These are features in [PNNL’s IM3 atlas](https://im3.pnnl.gov/visualizations); one campus can include several buildings. Place membership comes from Census boundaries, not postal names.

* **Albuquerque (Bernalillo County).** Searched the city’s [issued building permit layer](https://coageo.cabq.gov/cabqgeo/rest/services/agis/City_Building_Permits/FeatureServer/0) by work description. Four permits name a data center. Three telecom co-location records do not and were excluded. The city reports an issue date, without a current validity field. The [city air program](https://www.cabq.gov/airquality/air-quality-permits/do-i-need-an-air-quality-permit) separately publishes generator notifications; these stay on cards and never count as permits.
* **Los Lunas (Valencia County).** The [state's notice](https://www.env.nm.gov/public-notices/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2024/04/Legal-Notice-7026M6.pdf) identifies the Greater Kudu facility inside the village. The [agency's letter](https://cloud.env.nm.gov/resources/_translator.php/NjBkYWIyMGNkMGQyZGYyYzM3YmFhZjhhYl8xNzEwODc~.pdf) supplies two dates: application received and application ruled complete. The agency's preliminary intent is not a final decision.
* **Doña Ana County.** The [Project Jupiter docket](https://www.env.nm.gov/public-notices/yucca-growth-infrastructure-llc-ygi-microgrid/) tracks the replacement YGI application. The [applicant's transmittal filed with NMED](https://cloud.env.nm.gov/resources/_translator.php/ZWM3Njk5ZGQ3MjNjNzZiMjRmZmU1ZjgyMF8yMjYwMDk~.pdf) expressly links it to the data center and two earlier Acoma applications. The site is south of Santa Teresa; events are assigned to the county. English/Spanish copies, parts of the same draft and proposed issue dates do not create more permits.

The state [construction portal](https://www.rld.nm.gov/construction-industries/apply-for-a-permit/) remains a separate lookup. The municipal/state records collected here are not a complete statewide construction, air or stormwater register. No site-specific Santa Fe permit was established from the sources reviewed.

**Federal stormwater permits.** The [EPA download](https://echo.epa.gov/tools/data-downloads/icis-npdes-download-summary) joins each permit number to its facility, master permit, dated status and independently documented site. The seven published coverage records include three Project Jupiter microgrid permits, two Westpointe and two Greater Kudu. A power-company name containing “data center” did not establish that its project itself was a data center. EPA administers the construction general permit in the covered New Mexico area. These status counts describe that federal register at the cutoff.

To reconstruct the [state slice](../data/history/new_mexico/epa_pins.json) from a new copy of the EPA archive, run `PYTHONPATH=scripts python3 -c "from history_ms_la_epa import split; from pathlib import Path; split(Path('cache/history/ms/epa-national.tmp.zip'),states=('NM',))"`. Reconcile the national archive and state CSV checksums before updating the pinned edition. The multistate slicing run records additional states in the source manifest; the CSV slices for NM are the same.
