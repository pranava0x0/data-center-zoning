# Virginia: review of the statewide discovery hits

**November 15, 2026.** The [state directory](https://www.virginia.gov/local-government/) sweep saved **119 keyword occurrences in 22 documents for 13 of 324 directory entries**. Counts are from [the saved sweep output](../output/virginia_screen.json); they count occurrences, not distinct legal conclusions. The other 311 entries yielded no candidate in this bounded run or could not be retrieved. Those outcomes cannot establish the absence of law.

Of the 13 candidates, four were already represented. We reviewed the first five previously unrepresented candidates in sweep order (Botetourt, Brunswick, Chesterfield, Hanover and Henrico) and the explicit town-code hit for Troutville. **Five jurisdictions yielded eight new substantive provisions** in [sweep_promotions.json](../data/sweep_promotions.json). Hanover's current drafting project was held out. Three others have been triaged for later review. A promotion means that research identified a provision in a named government source. Whether the viewer can replay its evidence from a snapshot is independently reported in the release validation.

**What this tests.** A hit in an official source is a route to evidence. One mention in Henrico led to four operative provisions; eighteen in Botetourt led to one. A deterministic script can reliably relocate the published anchor but cannot tell whether a nearby number describes a local law, a recommendation, or one developer's agreement. The distinction belongs in reviewed data and in the viewer.
