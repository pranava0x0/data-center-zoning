# Elk Grove Village: from the parcel to the permit file

The [Cook County assessor's permit cards](https://assessorpropertydetails.cookcountyil.gov/datalets/datalet.aspx?LMparent=896&UseSearch=no&jur=016&mode=permit_ck_cc&pin=08224030160000&taxyr=2025) identify parcel **08224030160000, 1501 E Midway Court**. The applicant's Higgins Road mailing address is not the parcel address. The [collector](../scripts/collectors/illinois/elk_grove_village_cook_assessor.py) follows the register's Next links, verifies the parcel and page sequence, and retains the separate municipal permit and assessor record numbers. The tax roll year is not a cutoff for permit activity.

All 11 assessor cards were examined. Eight name Prime Data Center; a ninth explicitly describes a three-story data hall. These include temporary contractor trailers, electrical work and the data hall itself, not nine new data centers. Generic low-voltage work and a generator building filed under other applicant names remain unconfirmed. Seven entries have a municipal number, issue date and closed assessor record. They count as permits with unknown current validity; the assessor does not certify whether the village permit is still in force. Two pending entries with issue dates stay out of permit totals. An independently documented [village board resolution](https://files.illinoiscomptroller.gov/LocGovTIF/FY2023/01616532/23TIF01616532Busse_Elmurst_RPA_5.pdf#page=1) authorized a redevelopment agreement; it contributes no additional permit.
