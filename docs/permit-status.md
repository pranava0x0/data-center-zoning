# What a permit count means

The map counts **distinct identifiable permit instruments or coverage families in the collected sources**. Its status describes what an issuer reports about that instrument at the research snapshot, not whether a data center is operating or a proposed project is lawful. An unrecorded jurisdiction has *no observed permits in this collection*, not a verified count of zero permits in force.

| Map facet | Inclusion rule | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source reports active | An identified permit in a vetted register has an explicit active status. | Ohio EPA’s active construction coverage, not every issuance with a future program expiration date. |
| Historical | The source reports an expired, revoked, closed, finaled, terminated or historic permit under that register’s conventions. | A dated CGP termination or a finaled municipal building permit. A case approved years ago need not be historical in this sense. |
| Status unconfirmed | An instrument or permit-register identity can be identified, but its source only proves issuance or registry membership, records a modification, lacks present status, or contains an unresolved status conflict. | An issued Dallas building permit with no current status; this can be old or recent. Register membership alone does not guarantee issuance. |
| Other / non-counted events | Applications, planning decisions, registrations, non-permit determinations, unnumbered issuances and action-only reporting without a stable instrument identity. Unrecognized sources also fail closed here, with their own review flag. | Indiana’s state design release, a Texas accessibility registration marked “Project Closed,” a non-permit wetland case marked “Active,” or a Virginia air action lacking an instrument key. |

## Source and identity decisions

- **Virginia.** The [Loudoun issued-permits layer](https://logis.loudoun.gov/gis/rest/services/LMARC/LandMARC_Permits/MapServer/1) exposes separate issue, finalization and snapshot fields. A valid past finalization date is historical; an issue date is not a present active status. The monthly report and LandMARC share the county’s permit-number namespace. Submissions and land-use applications remain separate. [Prince William’s building layer](https://gisweb.pwcva.gov/arcgis/rest/services/Planning/Build_Out_Analysis/MapServer/9) distinguishes finaled, issued, planned and pending status. The [DEQ VWP fact sheet](https://gisdata.deq.virginia.gov/mapper_ext/DataFactSheets/Water_Permitting/General%20Permits_VWP_DEQFactSheet.pdf) explicitly describes historic coverage as no longer active; “Application” rows and the separate non-permit and delineation layers are excluded. For [DEQ construction stormwater](https://gisdata.deq.virginia.gov/mapper_ext/DataFactSheets/Water_Permitting/StormwaterPermitsDEQFactSheet.pdf), group exact CGP numbers across actions and locally administered records; distinguish documented termination from the general permit cycle date. An issue, transfer or renewal is not an explicit current active status. “Approved” on a [workflow](https://permits.virginia.gov/Permit/Search) is not an issuance. The [DEQ air action list](https://www.deq.virginia.gov/news-info/shortcuts/permits/air/issued-air-permits-for-data-centers) identifies the *site* and *application/action*, not a stable cross-revision instrument in this export. Retain the activity in history; do not count each amendment as another unique permit. See [air](history-air.md) and [water](history-water.md) source audits.
- **Ohio.** The [agency’s numbering guide](https://epa.ohio.gov/divisions-and-offices/surface-water/permitting/stormwater-program) explains that the alphabetic suffix denotes initial or renewed site coverage. Group AG and BG suffixes under the verified base and program; the highest observed issued version supplies status. The [agency GIS fields](https://geo.epa.ohio.gov/arcgis/rest/services/SurfaceWater/NPDES/MapServer/3) distinguish issue, effective and permit-status fields. A draft or withdrawn application with a newer suffix cannot displace a live permit. Expiration date conflicting with an active label means status unconfirmed, not automatic termination—[Ohio rules](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-administrative-code/rule-3745-33-03) allow continuation in specified circumstances. A [wastewater installation permit](https://epa.ohio.gov/divisions-and-offices/surface-water/permitting/wastewater-permit-to-install-pti-program) does not establish present discharge authority. Columbus closed building/trade permits differ from completed site-compliance filings. Planning entitlements and board determinations are non-permit events.
- **Texas.** [Austin’s issued construction register](https://data.austintexas.gov/Building-and-Development/Issued-Construction-Permits/3syk-w9eu) supplies current status; use its explicit Active, Final, VOID, Withdrawn or Expired on records already in the issued register. [Fort Worth’s development register](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/development-services/permits/issued) also contains applications. Finaled or expired permit status is historical; approved, pending, withdrawn, or void without independent evidence of issuance is not a counted permit. Repeated history for one number is grouped. [Dallas](https://www.dallasopendata.com/resource/e7gq-4sah.json) and San Antonio issued registers verify an issued permit; they do not alone establish it is still active. City numbering namespaces are separate. [TDLR registration](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/ab/abfaq.htm) is not a city building approval. An Abilene approved site-plan case is also not counted as an issued building permit.
- **Arizona.** Phoenix publishes separate [permit](https://maps.phoenix.gov/pub/rest/services/Public/Planning_Permit/MapServer/1) and [plan-review](https://maps.phoenix.gov/pub/rest/services/Public/Planning_Permit/MapServer/0) layers. Only permit-layer OPEN rows with an issue date, a term extending through the reporting date and no recorded completion enter source-active. A completed/expired permit is historic; open with conflicting or absent term evidence is unconfirmed. Work commencement/continuation can affect actual validity ([city inspection rules](https://www.phoenix.gov/administration/departments/pdd/tools-resources/inspections.html)). The compound type + number is the identity. Services and fire flow tests are excluded. [Mesa defines finaled date](https://data.mesaaz.gov/api/views/dzpk-hxfb) as the completed final inspection. Use it after verified issuance, not to promote a pre-issuance fee workflow. An occupancy certificate alone does not identify an issued construction permit. Goodyear's TCO or CO-or-final-inspection labels alone do not establish a definitive final disposition. [Maricopa's air register](https://gis.maricopa.gov/arcgis/rest/services/AQD/ImpactFacility/MapServer/0) separates facility operation, permit number/class and undated status code; no coded-value domain was supplied. Numbered Minor/Synthetic Minor entries have status unconfirmed, not assumed active or issued. Keep air notices and Pima enforcement outside permit totals. [ADEQ construction](https://services.arcgis.com/SzoH1oFM2apCSkx3/arcgis/rest/services/AZPDES/FeatureServer/3) and [industrial coverage](https://services.arcgis.com/SzoH1oFM2apCSkx3/arcgis/rest/services/AZPDES/FeatureServer/5) group the same number across dated approval/termination. A granted waiver is not a permit. A bare A/I activity code is not silently translated into a legal status.
- **Indiana.** The [IDHS project-activity register](https://www.in.gov/dhs/building-plan-review/project-activity-includes-real-time-plan-submissions-and-releases/) reports state plan applications and releases. The [state process](https://www.in.gov/dhs/building-plan-review/building-plan-review-process/) distinguishes local permits; an [official release in a state case record](https://www.in.gov/dhs/files/Petition-for-Review-IDHS-Construction-Design-Release-Project-No.-416937-Daniels-Vineyard.pdf) expressly says a release is not a building permit. “Standard” describes release type. Preserve filings and releases, not one inferred permit per project number. For IDEM air, the actual [original FESOP](https://permits.air.idem.in.gov/42502f.pdf) and [revision 089-45165-00639](https://permits.air.idem.in.gov/45165f.pdf) expressly reference F089-42502-00639; the revision resolves to its base number only while the inspected PDF hash matches. Other initial Title V/Part 70 or FESOP decisions count as identified permits with current status unconfirmed. Draft air permits, waterway deficiencies and hearing notices on the [state notice register](https://www.in.gov/idem/public-notices/public-notices-all-regions/) are not final issuance.

## Integration and limitations

```python
from permit_status import permit_metrics
by_id = permit_metrics(all_rows, as_of="2026-11-15")
cards = [{**r, **by_id[r["id"]]} for r in all_rows]
identified = [r for r in cards if r["permit_representative"]]
```

Compute annotations **before** filtering geographically. Each input event ID receives `permit_bucket`, `permit_key`, `permit_representative`, `permit_reason` `permit_event_type` and `permit_semantics_url` (the agency definition or register link). The original status, date role and number remain on the source card. Null keys never contribute to permit totals. For the state metric use representative rows or distinct keys. A county drill-down should count the same distinct keys it offers as cards; an unmapped site should not be imputed to a neighboring jurisdiction. For the complete action history the current group classification can differ from an older action’s historical status; present those as separate fields. Do not reconstruct past validity from a present-day register: the reporting cutoff is the **snapshot date**, not a time-travel switch. Known future actions are excluded.

Groups containing conflicting observations use a documented renewal sequence where available; otherwise differing statuses require comparable date evidence on every competing permit observation. Unorderable conflicts are unconfirmed. One representative survives ties deterministically, without treating the chosen ID as chronological evidence. Numbers are normalized only for case and surrounding whitespace; no general stripping of hyphens, slashes, permit suffixes or leading zeros. No grouping by place name, operator, address, parcel or facility name. Separate agencies can authorize work at the same site. Multiple counties can intersect a permit; do not add county counts as if geographically disjoint.

Run `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_permit_status.py'` for the counter’s regressions, then `python3 scripts/pipeline/validate_compass.py --strict-taxonomy` for full-source reconciliation and one-representative-per-key checks. The [crosscheck](../output/compass_crosscheck.json) reports counts, excluded reasons and unknown source/status combinations separately by state. A successful replay measures consistency with the cited snapshot; it does not establish statewide regulatory completeness.

[Scottsdale’s building register](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=68ba7b38073f4cd4aee38d2b59afcaf4) supplies separately numbered, issued site-linked electrical/plumbing permits: explicit FINALLED and EXPIRED are historical. Exact site/operator/parcel association does not establish the purpose of the work. [Hobart Plan Commission votes](https://www.cityofhobart.org/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Minutes/_05072026-2563) are planning decisions; a preliminary plat or favorable rezoning recommendation is not a final plat, rezoning or building permit.
