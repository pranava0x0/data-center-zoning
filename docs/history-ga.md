# Georgia: verified historical actions

At the November 16, 2026 cutoff the frozen export contains 109 dated actions from June 21, 1999 through October 23, 2026: **70 Georgia EPD-issued air instruments, two Alpharetta-issued building permits, 30 Atlanta Regional Commission project reviews, and seven scheduled local zoning requests**. Repeated appearances and permit revisions are intentional: these are actions, not counts of data centers. No current authorization, operational status or MW is inferred.

**Where we looked.** The November 2, 2026 [PNNL/IM3 mapped points](https://immm-sfa.github.io/datacenter-atlas/im3_datacenter_centroids.geojson) and [Census 2026 BAS incorporated-place geometry](https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/Places_CouSub_ConCity_SubMCD/MapServer/11) give 58 Georgia point records. Douglas (22) and Fulton (21) lead, ahead of Fayette (4); Cobb, Gwinnett and Newton have three apiece. Alpharetta (9), Atlanta (8) and Douglasville (6) lead incorporated places; 23 points fall outside an incorporated place. This is a spatial proxy, not an operating or planned facility inventory. Boundary simplification and campus point duplication can change local ranks. Exact table and inputs: `data/history/georgia/rank.json`. A county assignment on a permit requires evidence separate from the rank.

**State air records.** The [EPD Issued Air Permits register](https://air.gaepd.org/Permits) was searched by 16 facility-name phrases and permit-number fragment 7374. Predicates are in `data/history/georgia/config.json`. Pagination is followed; overlapping search results are deduplicated by full permit number. Among 81 numbered candidates, 70 have a data-center facility name or a project reference in that instrument’s narrative or permit. Older records with only an operator or industry-code match are held even if that operator has a later confirmed data-center permit. For example, “Vantage Industries” alone was not sufficient. The register's issuance date governs. The archive contains signed permit and narrative where linked. County must match both the AIRS county prefix and text on the first two pages of the same instrument or accompanying narrative. Earlier instruments do not inherit later locations. Revisions with different full numbers are separate instrument identities. Issuance does not mean an instrument remains active.

**Alpharetta.** The [city's commercial permits](https://alphagis.alpharetta.ga.us/arcgis/rest/services/OpenData/OpenData_PCE_Full/FeatureServer/1), land disturbance (layer 2) and tree (layer 4) layers were screened using names, site notes and an explicit address query. The public snapshots contain 392, 11 and 23 total features respectively. The two explicit matches are BC260265 (tenant) and BC260266 (landlord), both the ATL2 Phase 1B expansion, both with completed issue tasks on October 23, 2026. Date is the task completion timestamp converted to Georgia local time; issued status requires the completed issue task/result. Neither a blank land disturbance search nor 392 commercial features implies all historic city permits were available.

**Regional review.** Four keyword variants in the [Atlanta Regional Commission DRI search](https://atlantaregional.org/what-we-do/community-development/plan-reviews/) returned 34 distinct DRI review codes. Thirty had explicit project evidence. Four without sufficiently specific titles or supporting reports remain in `data/history/georgia/review.json`. These are regional assessments, not local entitlements. Full ARC register code is preserved, including older alphanumeric codes. Final-report dates are used where supported and no earlier than opening; otherwise the date is the register's opening date. The register's raw status is preserved even if a posted final report and search status appear out of step; neither implies local approval. Jurisdiction is the register’s county, which may differ from the submitting city. Mixed use proposals are included only when they include a data-center component.

**Local dockets.** Four selected [Douglas County agendas](https://douglascountyga.gov/DocumentCenter/View/11134/DRAFT-10-1-2024-Zoning-Agenda) (February 2024–February 2026) have six case appearances; TC Atlanta appears twice. These include special use permits, rezoning, impervious-surface relief and substations serving the requests. Cases grouped on a source agenda stay grouped, including the printed `S20214-51` spelling. South Fulton's [February 2, 2026 zoning information meeting](https://www.cityofsouthfultonga.gov/DocumentCenter/View/15137/CZIM-Agenda_02_2_26) documents Vantage case Z26-003. These dates only establish scheduled consideration. No resulting decision is asserted. The Douglas County GIS and Douglasville monthly-report index could not be retrieved under the collection policy. City of Atlanta, unincorporated Fulton and other cities' comprehensive building/trade registers have not been exhausted. This is an Atlanta-region-centered local sample; no absence inference is warranted.

**Reproduce.** From the project folder run `python3 scripts/collectors/georgia/history.py` (offline; pypdf required). `--fetch` fills missing source stages; for a fresh dated run use a separate workspace/snapshot and retain prior manifests. Hash-checked captures live in `cache/history/ga`, with query, city and permit-file manifests in `data/history/georgia/*`. The default run regenerates rank, holds, scope counts and `output/history/georgia/history.json`. `scripts/collectors/georgia/status.py` separates permit issuance, non-permit reviews, and requests.
