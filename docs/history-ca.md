# California permit and exemption history

The 2026-11-15 cutoff covers 404 events in eight source series. Only 244 are separately identifiable issued building/trade permits: 23 active, 201 historical and 20 with present status unconfirmed. The 404 includes environmental review and docket milestones that are not permits. It does not measure California's total inventory.

File 24-1198 is a useful check: two motions occurred; only one sustained the appeal and approved the conditional use permit. **Two dates matter.** The AVAIO Pittsburg order was adopted December 8, 2025 but posted several months later. The [official docket](https://efiling.energy.ca.gov/Lists/DocketLog.aspx?docketnumber=24-SPPE-01) and [final order](https://efiling.energy.ca.gov/GetDocument.aspx?tn=270673) permit that distinction. Every CEC decision row uses adoption date, with the docketed date in details. Application rows use the CEC docketing date of the main submission, or its accompanying cover-letter filing; this is not an approval or the date construction began. Reviewed dockets include McLaren, Laurelwood, Walsh, Sequoia, Mission College, Great Oaks South, CA3, San José, Lafayette, Bowers, Martin, STACK, Pittsburg and six proceedings without an admitted order. Coverage comes from the [CEC project list](https://www.energy.ca.gov/programs-and-topics/topics/power-plants/power-plant-compliance-and-siting), official linked docket pages, and earlier CEC decisions/meeting records. The 19 are a reviewed selection, not a claim of statewide exhaustion. Generator capacity is not IT load.

**Market priority.** The [PNNL physical inventory, pinned commit fe4d842](https://github.com/IMMM-SFA/datacenter-atlas/tree/fe4d8420f9fdc7b16b82b8461c5e4c39b93d8f1d) contains 114 California markers. Santa Clara County 74, Los Angeles 9, Alameda 7, Sacramento and San Diego 5 each, San Francisco 4. Santa Clara city leads with 53; San José has 14; Fremont, Los Angeles, San Diego, San Francisco and Sunnyvale tie at four. The [2024 Census place file](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/GENZ2024/shp/cb_2024_06_place_500k.zip) leaves four outside a mapped place; one belongs to Stanford CDP. The file history/california/rankings.csv covers all 14 counties with represented points and all matched places. County names/GEOIDs follow the [2024 Census gazetteer](https://www2.census.gov/geo/docs/maps-data/data/gazetteer/2024_Gazetteer/2024_Gaz_counties_national.zip). Counts are inventory markers, not planned campuses, utility queue positions or MW. Alameda has Hayward land use and statewide records, not comprehensive Fremont building coverage. Sacramento, San Diego, Sunnyvale, rural districts, environmental permits and closed cases in other cities are pending independent register acquisitions.

**How to update.** Run `python3 scripts/collectors/california/history.py` to build from the hashed local captures. `--refresh` fetches each configured source and rechecks primary rows/object IDs; a changed PDF decision, docket layout, source schema, unrecognized overlapping status, or undated action requires review. Go through data/history/california/config.json to admit new docket TN numbers and site-specific city matters; broad website search results, staff recommendations without action, unrelated municipal contracts and regional facilities of uncertain identity are not auto-admitted. Reselect city matters after refreshing as unreviewed title matches will be reported in history/california/exclusions.json. Socrata rows use explicit sector text in permit descriptions/use types. San José case numbers in the C.3 and planning layers use FOLDERRSN and preserve FOLDERNUM separately; issue dates are not universally construction start dates. California reuses Texas GIS/geometry helpers, the ID reconciliation applied in Arizona and Nevada, and local API-specific status mappings. Use city/project registers for parcel diligence, particularly where a description only identifies a supporting project or is missing sector terms.
