# Kentucky: permit-related local land-use histories

Eight verified actions: two Louisville Metro Category 3 development plans, each with a dated opening and approved status; a public commission hearing on the revised application; a state utility staff information request about the campus; and two undated, separately identified Oldham County withdrawal notices. **None of these is counted as an issued construction or environmental permit.** No federal site or NPDES records are repeated here.

[LOJIC’s metro planning register](https://gis.lojic.org/maps/rest/services/LojicSolutions/OpenDataDevelopment/MapServer/26) does not carry project names, so the lookup is by the exact case identifiers identified in the [metro inter-agency review report](https://content.govdelivery.com/attachments/KYLOUISVILLE/2026/02/12/file_attachments/3552263/IARC%20Report%20021026.pdf), p. 4. It states 25-CAT3-0015 is an update to 25-CAT3-0006 at 6001 Camp Ground Road; the former concerns 153 acres in M-3 and EZ-1. Stored status dates are the GIS register's status timestamps; they need not equal a public meeting date. The [March 5, 2026 recorded hearing](https://louisvilleky.new.swagit.com/events/45332) is separate. [Kentucky PSC question 4](https://psc.ky.gov/order_vault/Orders_2025/202500045_03272025.pdf) seeks support for proposed load estimates; it does not authorize the site or establish operating MW. [Oldham County](https://www.oldhamcountyky.gov/projectlincoln) posted the two Project Lincoln withdrawals; their linked file host did not clear robots preflight. No withdrawal date or new identifier was inferred.

The 2026-11-02 [PNNL/IM3 snapshot](https://immm-sfa.github.io/datacenter-atlas/im3_datacenter_centroids.geojson) has only six Kentucky point records: Jefferson two; Boone, Floyd, Hopkins and McCracken one each. The file in `data/history/kentucky/rank.json` also ranks [Census BAS 2026](https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/Places_CouSub_ConCity_SubMCD/MapServer/11) incorporated places. Sites are deliberately not ranked by the indicative project load in the PSC filing. Other local building and environmental series, including counties with single real-world sites, are gaps. Louisville’s air-board page and the state energy-plan portal were unavailable to this automated collection.

Run `python3 scripts/collectors/kentucky/history.py` for offline replay or `--fetch` for missing source stages. Source URLs, row-status and page assertions, hash manifests and explicit failures are in `data/history/kentucky/*`; the raw responses are in `cache/history/ky`. This source adapter also drives the KS and ND releases.
