# Virginia pilot: read the parcel, then the project

Research cutoff: **November 15, 2026 (Eastern)**. This is a targeted pilot, not an inventory of all Virginia localities or an eligibility determination for a site. The Virginia research file contains selected provisions for the Commonwealth, Loudoun, Fairfax, Prince William and James City counties, the Town of Leesburg, the independent cities of Fredericksburg and Chesapeake, and the Loudoun Water service area. See [the records](../data/virginia.json) for individual locators and evidence. Other files in the release may add jurisdictions or authorities. Authority coverage means that some evidence was found, not that every topic was reviewed.

## Loudoun: why one color per county can mislead

1. **Choose the authority and ordinance vintage.** Loudoun's [ordinance portal](https://www.loudoun.gov/zoningordinance) excludes the seven incorporated towns, identifies certain commercial/industrial Route 28 parcels still under the 1972 ordinance, and identifies grandfathered cases under the Revised 1993 ordinance. A property with a Leesburg postal address need not be inside Leesburg town limits. Check [county WebLogis](https://logis.loudoun.gov/weblogis/) and the parcel's approval instruments. The Route 28 tax district, legacy optional overlay footprint and recorded election are three different concepts. A protected 1972 parcel and a parcel with a recorded optional-overlay election are not interchangeable.
2. **Resolve the district and overlay.** The [March 2025 adopted amendment](https://www.loudoun.gov/6221/Phase-1-Project-Plan-for-Data-Center-Sta) changed the IP/GI/MR-HI data-center entries to special exception; OP also displays S in the [office/industrial use table, row 101](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/loudouncounty-va-zo/ereader/ereader/files/basic-html/page202.html). In contrast, [§ 5.09.01(A)](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/loudouncounty-va-zo/ereader/ereader/files/basic-html/page379.html) limits the legacy optional overlay to parcels with qualifying prior elections, and [table 5.09.06-2](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/loudouncounty-va-zo/ereader/ereader/files/basic-html/page408.html) lists CO = special exception; CB and CI = permitted subject to district standards. Neither table decides whether a given parcel actually enjoys those rights. The electronic reader has a misleading “SC” browser title; the document body identifies Loudoun County, VA and the county links to the publisher. A district's P designation does not dispense with site, building, fire or environmental approvals.
3. **Read the grandfathering instrument, not just its headline.** The [signed resolution](https://www.loudoun.gov/DocumentCenter/View/215835/SIGNED-Data-Center-CPAM-ZOAM-Grandfathering-Resolution) was inspected as five page images. In ¶¶ 1–3 and 10–12, the acceptance cutoff includes **February 12, 2025**; the county's shorter [phase overview](https://www.loudoun.gov/6221/Phase-1-Project-Plan-for-Data-Center-Sta) says “prior to.” Official acceptance, application type, material changes and diligent pursuit matter. The more-than-500-ft test applies to the administrative route: proposed structures and external mechanical equipment relative to residential structures existing at the cutoff. Paragraph 3 permits revisions to satisfy that test. The [searchable pathway guide](https://www.loudoun.gov/DocumentCenter/View/215836) corroborates it and explicitly defers to the signed instrument. Even after protection is lost, [table notes 3–4](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/loudouncounty-va-zo/ereader/ereader/files/basic-html/page205.html) and a separate vested-rights determination may affect what needs a new SPEX. Acceptance of an application is not approval to build.
4. **Check the project-specific studies and separate permits.** Evaluate the [data-center use standards](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/loudouncounty-va-zo/ereader/ereader/files/basic-html/page283.html), [commercial construction/occupancy checklist](https://www.loudoun.gov/4265/Residential-and-Commercial-Construction-), [local grading requirements](https://www.loudoun.gov/6244/Land-Disturbance) and [Fire Marshal program](https://www.loudoun.gov/4342/Fire-Prevention-Permits). The general grading screen is 5,000 ft² disturbed, with separate small-disturbance checks in sensitive areas. Virginia [construction stormwater criteria](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title9/agency25/chapter875/section470/) apply at one acre including cumulative common-plan disturbance; coverage and registration are described by [DEQ](https://www.deq.virginia.gov/water/stormwater/stormwater-construction). These are independent screens with different units and authorities.
5. **Ask how the facility is powered, cooled and drained.** Virginia's [HEUF law](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title15.2/chapter22/section15.2-2209.5/) covers certain legislative applications for facilities reasonably expected to require at least 100 MW from a retail utility; its 500-ft distance sets a **sound-study receptor radius**, not a building setback. Check the subsection F expansion provision rather than applying the new-facility screen to every addition. [Loudoun's county notice](https://www.loudoun.gov/Blog.aspx?IID=1591) requires the additional local resource assessment that the state allowed localities to elect. Generator emissions are a separate matter: [Va. Code § 10.1-1322.6](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title10.1/chapter13/section10.1-1322.6/) sets a Tier 4-equivalent emissions ceiling for gen-sets under covered permit applications from July 1, 2026. Do not translate this performance requirement into a statutory mandate to purchase a named product. [DEQ memo 2025-03, p. 3](https://www.deq.virginia.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/27426/638730560926670000) distinguishes separate server-building work from starting construction on the regulated generators themselves.
6. **Distinguish withdrawal and discharge.** A direct well, direct surface intake, municipal or reclaimed supply, noncontact cooling discharge and discharge to a sewer pose distinct questions. See [groundwater permit exclusions](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title62.1/chapter25/section62.1-259/), the [surface withdrawal exclusion and override](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title9/agency25/chapter210/section310/), [noncontact cooling GP](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title9/agency25/chapter196/section70/) and [Loudoun Water pretreatment](https://www.loudounwater.org/industrial-pretreatment-program-faq). A zoning table's “industrial” label does not make the facility a Significant Industrial User of a sewer. For the 25,000-gal/day volume criterion, exclude sanitary, noncontact cooling and boiler blowdown wastewater; other independent criteria can still qualify the user. This utility's service area is not represented as the entire county.

## What changes outside Loudoun?

|---|---|
| [Leesburg, art. 8, § 8.6.4](https://www.leesburgva.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/4542/638908610481770000) | The town independently lists data centers and related electrical substations for special-exception review in PEC. The county result cannot be inherited from the county map. |
| [Fairfax, enacted 2024 amendment](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/planning-development/data-centers) | Size threshold by building and district: over 40,000 ft² in C-3/C-4 or over 80,000 ft² in I-2/I-3/I-4 needs a special exception. The usual 200-ft building and 300-ft ground-equipment residential setbacks have exceptions; ground equipment can use the building as separation. Assess noise both before site plan approval and after construction; the final pages contain transition rules for older applications. |
| [Prince William, Ord. 26-67](https://www.pwcva.gov/department/planning-office/dpa2026-00006-data-center-opportunity-zone-overlay-district-dcozod) | September 22, 2026 restructuring retained some prior by-right permissions via five eligibility paths. The agency states that the last pending-application path closes December 21, with a revised map **after** that. Outside, only the enumerated districts and designated land bays use the stated SUP path. Historical proposals and old polygons on the very same page do not establish present rights. |
| [James City, Ord. 31A-364](https://documents.jamescitycountyva.gov/Minutes/Ordinances/2025/31A-36409092025.pdf) | Adopted markup removed commercial “data processing centers” entries and inserted industrial data centers by SUP in M-2. Accessory IT and cryptocurrency mining are excluded from the data-center definition. Plain PDF extraction can preserve deleted text; inspect redlines/tables. The [separate Board policy](https://documents.jamescitycountyva.gov/Minutes/AdoptedResolutions/2025/November/Adpt_DataCtrFacrevres11122025.pdf) recommends distances from the fenced area and informs case conditions. Do not publish it as a mandatory setback. |
| [Fredericksburg, § 72-34.9](https://ecode360.com/49622412) | By-right data-center campuses in the mapped TOD require at least 150 contiguous acres. Restrictions on permanent use of potable water depend on the underlying district. When appropriate, temporary use requires a Council-approved water services agreement; direct groundwater, quarry water and raw river water are barred for data-center cooling within the overlay. No citywide by-right inference. |
| [Chesapeake, adopted ordinance and timeline](https://www.cityofchesapeake.net/3783/Data-Center-Ordinance-and-Policy) | City describes a CUP in M-1/M-2 with a distinct by-right route in CoVAC; public water and approved public wastewater service still apply. A hearing pause and an inquiry about possible authority to ban the use are separate events; neither proves that all projects are banned. |

## Change watch and verification boundaries

* **Loudoun:** the Board approved a *work plan* for [Phase 2](https://www.loudoun.gov/6222/Phase-2-Data-Center-Standards-Locations), not its proposed text. The [September announcement](https://www.loudoun.gov/m/newsflash/home/detail/10874) describes a vote to consider a pause. [County-posted meeting slides](https://www.loudoun.gov/DocumentCenter/View/222881/Deimos-Slides) say the resolution was deferred to November 17, after this cutoff. Do not promote the proposal or characterize it as an operative countywide moratorium without later Board action.
* **Leesburg:** monitor the [rewrite calendar](https://www.leesburgva.gov/Home/Components/Calendar/Event/38943/). A tentative adoption date is not proof of enactment. Compare a signed replacement, effective date and transition clauses before updating the older PEC provision.
* **Virginia water cutoff test:** the DEQ [overview](https://www.deq.virginia.gov/water/water-withdrawal/surface-water) uses “more than” for the withdrawal quantities. The [actual regulation, A(11)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title9/agency25/chapter210/section310/) defines an exclusion for amounts **less than** each number. At precisely 10,000 gal/day nontidal, that exclusion alone therefore does not settle permit status; check other exclusions and DEQ's authority in B.
* **Identity:** independent cities use county-equivalent geographic identifiers here; Leesburg uses an incorporated-place identifier ([Census geography](https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/tigerwebmain/Files/bas25/tigerweb_bas25_incplace_2024_acs24_va.html)). Loudoun Water has no county GEOID assigned. Mapping county or state records shows where an authority applies in part; it is not zoning polygon or service-boundary proof.
* **Access and evidence:** an anchor is an exact short search key with whitespace normalization; the locator, surrounding clauses and linked documents establish meaning. Browser-indexed public text can be observable even when a compliant automated request is blocked, returns an error or lacks a usable text layer. Preserve that review status. Do not infer the requirement disappeared. County's signed grandfathering scan was visually read; a searchable agency pathway guide is the text anchor and the signed resolution is the controlling document.
* **Not inventoried:** district-by-district rules in every Virginia locality, all legacy parcel elections, town boundary layers, the history of each use table, private restrictions, site-specific proffers and permits, connection availability, all local fire thresholds, all wetlands/stream effects and federal approvals. A permit issued for one facility does not establish a general siting requirement. Never infer “no restriction” from missing research.

## Small acceptance cases for future ingests

These are expected interpretations, not site-specific decisions:

| Inputs | What a correct viewer or query should return |
|---|---|
| Unincorporated Loudoun, ordinary IP parcel, new data center, earlier approval not established | Special exception; prompt to check prior rights and other permits. |
| Parcel inside Leesburg, PEC | Town rules for land use; do not assign Loudoun's IP rule. Other regulators’ programs may still apply. |
| Administrative Loudoun site plan accepted on February 12, 2025; 500 ft exactly | Inclusive filing-date check passes, strict **greater-than** distance check fails on the information given. Check a revision, the resolution and independent approval paths. |
| Covered new HEUF, exactly 100 MW; home at 500 ft | Sound-assessment screen applies; the distance does not imply a mandatory 500-ft building setback. |
| 0.6 acre disturbed in a larger 2-acre common development in unincorporated Loudoun | Independently screen county grading and statewide construction stormwater. |
| James City County project within 1,000 ft of an occupied home | Record the Board's recommendation and SUP process; no automatic denial solely from that policy distance. |
