# South Dakota project records

South Dakota has two observed PNNL centroids, both in Minnehaha County, one inside Sioux Falls and one outside incorporated places. These are discovery priorities. McPherson County has no mapped observation in this snapshot, but has a documented proposed project. Five published actions trace Leola. Sioux Falls is a ranked collection gap. None establishes an issued building permit.

Sioux Falls’ [January 6 city council minutes](https://amv.siouxfalls.gov/OnBaseAgendaOnline/Meetings/ViewMeeting?doctype=2&id=4182) and the planning packet could not be archived directly from the government site. The Gemini rezoning and preliminary subdivision plan are therefore withheld from the downloadable history.

Leola filed for electric provider assignment, distinct from local land-use permission. The [PUC docket](https://puc.sd.gov/Dockets/Electric/2024/EL24-027.aspx) records August 2024 filing, a September 8, 2026 [commission vote](https://puc.sd.gov/minutes/2026/0908.aspx) and September 11 [settlement order](https://puc.sd.gov/commission/dockets/electric/2024/EL24-027/EL24-027SettleStip.pdf). The state docket also archives the [county-form conditional-use application and supplement](https://puc.sd.gov/commission/dockets/electric/2024/EL24-027/CondUsePermit.pdf) and [unapproved county minutes](https://puc.sd.gov/commission/dockets/electric/2024/EL24-027/ExhE010925.pdf), identifying 24-CUP-04 and discussion of deferral. The printed word “permit” in the state exhibit title does not convert an unsigned decision sheet into an issued instrument. A [November 2026 county post](https://mcpherson.sdcounty.gov/mcphersoncountysdmeetings/) links a later packet 26-CUP-04; its external folder disallowed retrieval. The post alone does not establish its disposition or name the applicant, so it is held out of the case count.
