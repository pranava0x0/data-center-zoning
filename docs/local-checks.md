# Local controls: nine local jurisdictions and a change-of-law check

Selected local authorities and contrasts as of November 15, 2026. The accompanying [records](../data/extra_local.json) are not a census. For each claim they identify the issuer, level, GEOID, section, short source anchor and approval/status type. None of these governments is presumed to regulate the full surrounding county. A source anchor is a way to find the passage; read the full cited sections and referenced tables for the legal test.

| Where | What an extractor must preserve | Check against primary sources |
|---|---|---|
| Boone County, IN | The operative text states the beginning and end of a temporary pause. Keep that separate from the signature and state-law commencement clause. The county's unincorporated zoning jurisdiction and the exclusion for on-campus internal IT limit the reach. | [Ordinance 2026-08, pp. 1–3](https://boonecounty.in.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Ordinance-2026-08.Moratorium-on-data-centers.pdf); [Area Plan Commission ordinance list](https://boonecounty.in.gov/offices/area-plan/ordinance-downloads/). §§2–3 specify June 16, 2026 through June 15, 2027. Do not calculate the end by adding one year to a guessed signature date. |
| Parke County, IN | Closed-loop, non-evaporating cooling **and** bulk water brought to the site. Simply categorizing this “closed loop” misses the separate source restriction. A small accessory server room falls outside the defined use. | [Ordinance 2026-11, ¶¶1–2, p. 2](https://www.in.gov/counties/parke/files/2026/Data-Center-Ordinance-2026-11.pdf). The screens here do not resolve site-specific zoning, including whether municipal jurisdiction applies. |
| Warren County, IN | Closed loop is the default. The commissioners may accept an alternative shown to use less water **and** less energy; an applicant relying on nonpublic water must submit follow-up studies. | [Ordinance 2026-0803B, §§1–2](https://www.warrencounty.in.gov/Documents/How%20Do%20I/Documents%20and%20Forms/Government%20Document%20Center/Ordinances/2026/Ordinance%202026-0803B%20Amending%20Regulation%20of%20Siting%20Data%20Centers%20Concerning%20Water%20Use.pdf?t=202608171342270). An amendment expressly leaves the rest of the March ordinance in force. |
| San Marcos, TX | The adopted June 16 amendment bars the defined use in city zoning districts. It is not the January or March draft, a countywide ban, or evidence about an individual vested project. | [Council action, item 17](https://www.sanmarcostx.gov/4772/June-16-2026-City-Council-Recap); [official development code and June 16 effective edition](https://sanmarcostx.gov/1289/Codes-Ordinances). |
| Talty, TX | The approved ordinance caption establishes a conditional-use pathway. It does not supply a district list or numeric standards. A notice about a nearby project on the same page explicitly says the city no longer has oversight of its site. | [City publication, September 16 and November 12 updates](https://www.taltytexas.com/page/article/76). Keep the municipal rule and project jurisdiction as two separate questions. |
| Lewisville, TX | A special use permit is discretionary; applications face planning commission and city council hearings. The city's June approval announcement lists the industrial/warehouse districts and residential prohibition. | [City council approval announcement](https://www.cityoflewisville.com/Home/Components/News/News/8405/); [adopted ordinance 0847-26-ORD](https://library.municode.com/tx/lewisville/ordinances/unified_development_code?nodeId=1433317). Do not interpret water/electrical capacity considerations as numeric withdrawal permits. |
| North Richland Hills, TX | The 2025 table is real but no longer governs new facilities. The **10,000 sq ft** boundary determines which set of districts is eligible; the July 2026 change requires council special-use approval for both sizes. A project-specific discussion of a previously allowed facility appears on the same city page. | [July amendment notice](https://www.nrhtx.com/m/newsflash/home/detail/1333); [size-specific city explanation](https://www.nrhtx.com/1226/What-People-Are-Talking-About); [codified table, including Ordinance 3965](https://ecode360.com/46062777). Test the boundary at exactly 10,000 and just above; keep the project-specific “by right” explanation out of new-application results. |
| Marana, AZ | New principal-use projects require an appropriate adopted specific plan. The town water provider's cooling restriction must not be widened into a ban on every potable-water use or all potable-water providers. Preserve plan-specific waivers if reviewing a parcel. | [Town explanation](https://www.maranaaz.gov/Government/Data-Centers); [ordinance exhibit](https://www.maranaaz.gov/files/assets/cityofmarana/v/1/town-clerk/documents/ordinances/ordinance-2024.029-adoptiong-amendments-to-marana-town-code-title-17-_land-development_-revising-section-17-1-6-definitions.pdf); [Town Code §17-6-13(A), (C), (H)](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/maranaaz/latest/marana_az/0-0-0-17317). |
| Mesa, AZ | A base district is insufficient for a new principal use under the industrial table: council must authorize it when approving a PAD overlay. Parking uses incremental brackets, not the second rate for the entire property above 200,000 sq ft. | [Ordinance 5957, §§2 and 5](https://apps.mesaaz.gov/meetingarchive/ArchiveDocuments/Ordinances/Intro%20Ord%205957%20-%20Mesa%20City%20Code%20-%20Data%20Centers.pdf); [city bulletin confirming adoption](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/AZMESA/bulletins/3f359d3). The bulletin repeats “GI” for Heavy Industrial; the legal table explicitly uses **HI**. Verify waivers or pre-existing rights for individual sites. |

**Primary documents deliberately not converted into current requirements:** Spencer County's [Ordinance 2026-07 file](https://www.in.gov/counties/spencer/files/commissioners/Ordinance-2026-07-Data-Centers.pdf) has a numbered, detailed instrument but blank signature blocks. A county-domain PDF alone does not establish adoption. Nye County's [August adoption notice](https://www.nyecountynv.gov/m/newsflash/Archive/Item/1530?arcId=2680) links a [bill text with geographic restrictions](https://www.nyecountynv.gov/DocumentCenter/View/51626/Item8); the county subsequently published an [October hearing on a different data-center permitting chapter](https://www.nyecountynv.gov/m/NewsFlash/Home/Detail/1538). Reconcile both final adopted texts, amendments made in session, and litigation before coding the geographic extent of a current prohibition; do not publish the August attachment as a countywide current blanket ban.
