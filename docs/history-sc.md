# South Carolina: air construction and groundwater

Busiest counties by the 16 PNNL physical-site markers: Berkeley 9, Spartanburg 2, five others one each. Twelve markers are outside incorporated places and CDPs. Google has several mapped structures at a single Berkeley campus; these counts do not imply nine operators or permits.

The [December 2019 DHEC board minutes](https://dph.sc.gov/sites/scdph/files/media/document/Board%20Minutes/2019-12-12-Minutes.pdf#page=9) name groundwater withdrawal permit 08IN015, source 08IN015G01 and review docket 19-RFR-48 for Maguro (Google). The challenge was withdrawn under a consent agreement; the board received it as information. This is not permit revocation. The underlying signed permit, issue date, quantity and term are not inferred from the minutes. A digest-bound manual visual check verifies the scanned number.

The [Gannett decision and statement of basis](https://des.sc.gov/sites/des/files/Documents/BAQ/DepartmentDecisions/GannettEnterprises/DepartmentDecision.pdf) and [Valara project docket](https://des.sc.gov/community/community-engagement/environmental-sites-projects/valara-holdings-high-performance-compute-center) supply three signed, separately numbered synthetic minor air construction permits and their application/comment milestones. Valara's second number covers different equipment and is not assumed to replace the first. An air construction decision does not establish an active operating permit. The 15 events are a bounded case sample. The [operating permit search](https://apps.des.sc.gov/PermitCoverage) returned an application error for Maguro; Berkeley’s [capital projects service](https://gis.berkeleycountysc.gov/arcgis/rest/services/buildingberkeley/buidling_berkeley/MapServer) describes public works, not the private building register. These are coverage gaps, not negative findings.
