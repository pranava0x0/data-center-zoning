# Arkansas: a statewide permit screen and one municipal register

At the November 15, 2026 cutoff, 23 source events survived screening. [Arkansas DEQ's PDS extracts](https://adeq.state.ar.us/home/pdssql/pds.aspx) contained 32,882 facility rows, 62,713 permit rows and 68,961 joined summary rows. Match the explicit sector (NAICS 518210 or SIC 7374), data-center terms or documented aliases to facility records, then join their agency facility identifiers to the permit and [state air records](https://adeq.state.ar.us/air/permits/applications.aspx). Twelve candidate facility identities yielded four with air records. Some sites include dedicated power or ancillary equipment. A match is not proof of an operating computing campus.

Twelve rows describe numbered air permits and application stages, four describe other air requests, six are tank program entries without an issuance date, and one is a closed Little Rock electrical permit. The signed [2507-AOP-R0](https://adeq.state.ar.us/downloads/WebDatabases/PermitsOnline/Air/2507-AOP-R0.pdf) supports issuance; receipt and administrative completeness of its next revision do not establish issuance. For an undated other-air-request disposition, the agency label “Issued” does not establish a Title V operating permit. Two hazardous-waste records could reflect a former manufacturer and remain held. Three water identifiers were left to the separate national NPDES register to avoid double counting.

The [Little Rock municipal GIS](https://maps.littlerock.gov/server/rest/services/Permits/MapServer) returned no open matching rows and 19 matching closed fee rows for two distinct numbers. The crypto-only permit was held; 202219031 was included. This is not a complete count of city permits: filings without a matched description or reliable alias would be missed. Searcy/White County was also researched, without a confirmed local case for this release.

Pulaski and Johnson counties have six collected events each, Benton and Crittenden have five each, and Washington has one. These ranks describe evidence collected, not market size. For state-reported locality assignments, do not assume the state agency's location proves city zoning jurisdiction. Separately, [Conway O-23-72](https://media.conwayarkansas.gov/media/archive/ordinances/2023/O-23-72.pdf) adds a data-center use table and noise submissions; it is in the ordinance layer, not a permit event.
