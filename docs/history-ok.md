# Oklahoma: a purpose-built state register

**38 dated actions** as of November 16, 2026: 24 state-issued permits or permit-by-rule registrations, four final air applicability determinations, nine permit applications and one local rezoning staff recommendation. The 24 are instrument identities, not sites. Fourteen are air construction permits (including modification numbers), three air permit-by-rule registrations, two air operating permits, one Title V operating permit, two waterline construction permits, one sewer-line permit and one industrial wastewater permit. This register yielded historical actions back to 2010. No issuance alone proves operation or ongoing validity.

The [Oklahoma DEQ consolidated data-center index](https://oklahoma.gov/deq/deq-information-on-data-centers.html) explicitly covers air, land and water but excludes construction stormwater. Its 39 substantive rows contain 37 distinct supportable cases. The sequence of the original table is retained. A Roland/Sequoyah row links to the Muskogee/Webbers Falls instrument: it is held and not mapped to Sequoyah. A second Muskogee row repeats the same instrument and is deduplicated. Names published by DEQ are preserved, even unusual spacing. For issued identifiers, the individual agency documents take precedence; for pending files, use the individual public review page’s status and receipt date, or the active state register’s explicitly labeled status and last-status date if the case page fails. State [annual issue](https://oklahoma.gov/deq/permits/air-permits/pp-ip/air-permits-issued-during-past-year.html) and [active application](https://oklahoma.gov/deq/permits/air-permits/pp-ip/active-air-permit-listing.html) CSVs were captured. Dates inferred solely from file names are not allowed. When only a technical review memorandum or transmittal letter is dated, the role identifies that, not issuance.

The [PNNL/IM3 mapped data](https://immm-sfa.github.io/datacenter-atlas/im3_datacenter_centroids.geojson) contain 11 Oklahoma points: Tulsa County eight, Bryan, Muskogee and Oklahoma counties one each. Seven points are within Tulsa corporate limits using Census BAS 2026 boundaries. These are mapped records, not unique operational sites or planned capacity. The Tulsa Planning Office's [Project Anthem rezoning staff report](https://tulsaplanning.org/tmapc/agendas/exhibits/Z-7851.pdf) recommends denial of AG-to-IL rezoning; the cover prints a March 18 hearing and also says it was continued. No local decision is inferred. Tulsa-wide city building/trade coverage remains incomplete.

Run `python3 scripts/collectors/oklahoma/history.py` for hash-checked offline regeneration; `--fetch` fills missing stages, `--ocr` regenerates reviewed first-page/second-page OCR from frozen input on macOS. The normalized output, raw-case review reasons, census/atlas ranks and manifests distinguish environmental approvals from local actions. State-issued OPDES permit OK0100439 shares the national NPDES identity namespace. Individual public water/sewer line permits are not treated as industrial discharge permits.
