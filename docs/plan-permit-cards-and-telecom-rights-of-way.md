# Next session: permit cards, jurisdiction collectors and telecom rights-of-way

Planning handoff, November 16, 2026. Start in `local ordinance zoning`. Another session uses this workspace: inspect current inputs, collector registry, routes and release results before editing. The examples below are acceptance cases, not new published records.

## 1. Make the existing cards answer “Which approval, from whom, and when?”

Keep the cards. Put the instrument's name first, the responsible office and its **role** next, then a sentence explaining who needs it, what triggers it and when. Keep the source link at the top. Put citations by section/page, exact quotations, exceptions and timeline detail one disclosure away. Never clip the decision, threshold or timing behind an ellipsis.

For an ordinance provision, name the approval or the specific standard. For a history entry, use the original document title when supplied; otherwise use the agency's record type and identifier. Put the project or applicant underneath, not in place of the title. Label a synthesized description as such in the data. When a source only names the publisher, don't promote it to issuer: say which role is unreported. Separate the office that accepts filings, technical reviewer, recommending body and final decision-maker; only surface the relevant ones on the compact card.

Keep different meanings of time separate: filing prerequisite, clock start, estimated processing time, statutory deadline and actual dated action. Preserve date precision and original status text. A meeting date is not an effective date, an approval is not evidence of current validity, and a completed application is not proof that construction finished. Never invent a permit number or duration.

**First four fixtures:** Fairfax County's special exceptions for commercial and industrial districts, noise study and residential setbacks. Example of the intended first card:

> **Special Exception — data center over 40,000 sq ft in C-3 or C-4**  
> **Decision:** Fairfax County Board of Supervisors, after a Planning Commission hearing and recommendation.  
> Applies to gross floor area per building. The county estimates about six months from official acceptance of the application.

Verify the parallel 80,000 sq ft threshold in I-2/I-3/I-4 separately. For noise, name the Zoning Administrator or designee and identify the two submission points: before site-plan approval and before the post-construction Nonresidential Use Permit. For setbacks, lead with the distance standard, then identify when a Special Exception is necessary to reduce it. Don't call either underlying standard a permit. Review the existing draft `data/fairfax_card_evidence.json` against the [adopted amendments](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/planning-development/data-centers), [decision process](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/planning-development/zoning/special-exception-process), [processing estimate](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/planning-development/zoning/application-acceptance), [noise study instructions](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/planning-development/sites/planning-development/files/Assets/Documents/zoning/data-centers/Noise-Study-Requirements.pdf) and [2026 amendment for qualifying facilities](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/planning-development/sites/planning-development/files/Assets/Documents/zoning%20ordinance/adopted%20amendments/ZO-112_2-2026-22.pdf). Apply effective dates and prior-approval provisions; don't apply new requirements retroactively to old permits.

After Fairfax, test Loudoun building, zoning and occupancy approvals, including town-specific zoning offices ([county explanation](https://www.loudoun.gov/2851/Building-Development---Permits)). Audit the rest of the atlas by source series. Carry the identity fields through cards, keyword search, CSV and permit details. Report how many records have an official title, a source type only, a named issuer or an unresolved role; assign unresolved cases to a review queue. Don't silently convert all  rules into permits or all historical events into approvals.

## 2. Organize collectors by the authority and geography a person would look for

Extend the existing `data/jurisdiction_registry.json` and `scripts/pipeline/jurisdiction.py`; avoid a second independent registry. Generate a lookup by full state and jurisdiction name, aliases and Census GEOID where available. Each entry should list source owner, geographic scope, actual collector/config path, records produced, refresh command and offline replay command. Separate project location from issuer: a state permit for a Loudoun project must be discoverable under both.

Proposed filenames: `collectors/virginia/fairfax_county/fairfax_county_utility_operations_permits.py`, `collectors/virginia/leesburg/leesburg_right_of_way_permits.py`, and `collectors/virginia/statewide/virginia_vdot_land_use_permits.py`. These are proposed routes, not delivered scripts. Only create a collector when there is an accessible record source; a checklist alone gets a requirements entry. Keep shared code named for its real format (ArcGIS pagination, meeting minutes, document receipts). A common statewide register should be fetched once, with explicit locality filters or a documented shared snapshot.

Trace multi-area scripts such as `westmetro.py` and `metropriority.py` to their actual inputs, outputs and callers. Split unrelated collection paths. Preserve old commands as compatibility entry points during migration. A single-jurisdiction refresh must not overwrite another jurisdiction's records. Test offline replay twice plus sibling-file checksums before retiring any old entry point.

## 3. Pilot fiber and telecom rights-of-way in Virginia

Begin with Loudoun County, Leesburg and Fairfax County plus VDOT. For each job, establish **who controls the road, easement or pole** before assigning an issuer. A mailing address cannot answer that question. Keep fiber/telecom separate from data centers in filters, counts and exports. Only label a permit as serving a data center when an official record links the projects. Preserve standalone broadband work as telecom history.

| Starting source | What the pilot should establish |
|---|---|
| [VDOT land use permits](https://www.vdot.virginia.gov/doing-business/technical-guidance-and-support/land-use-and-development/land-use-permits/) and [Virginia Permit Transparency search](https://permits.virginia.gov/Permit/Search) | Identify state-maintained ROW, permit class, applicant, authority, issue decision, and any explicitly published expiration/closure. Distinguish one installation from district-wide authority covering multiple service connections. |
| [Loudoun: VDOT application 949:61269](https://permits.virginia.gov/Permit/Details/98688) | Positive history fixture. Show the source type **Utility - Fiber Optic**, VDOT, and its description of conduit and fiber work along Bolyn Road. The state lists receipt on August 21, 2025 and determination on October 19, 2026. The original target, revised target and actual decision are different fields. The source calls 949:61269 the application number. Preserve its status; do not infer active construction, current validity or a data-center customer. Recheck the live record and archive it before publication. |
| [Leesburg right-of-way permits](https://www.leesburgva.gov/departments/public-works/traffic-management-services/right-of-way-permits) | Town-controlled streets: underground cables/conduit, borings, overhead installations. Find the public history separately; a form or email submission channel proves a process, not an issued permit. |
| [Fairfax Utility Company Operations Permit (UP)](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/landdevelopment/node/785) | County-controlled land/easements and qualifying roads not yet accepted into VDOT's system. Check the approved site plan, land control and application conditions. A Fairfax address alone does not establish county permitting authority. |
| [Fairfax hearing advertisement](https://video.fairfaxcounty.gov/MinutesViewer.php?clip_id=3310&view_id=7) and [later board action](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/boardofsupervisors/sites/boardofsupervisors/files/Assets/documents/05-21-24-Final-Summary.pdf) | Negative counting fixture: the 6900 Newington Road fiber easement has multiple governmental actions. Record their sequence separately. Board authorization to convey does not prove execution or recording of a deed, or issuance of a street-opening permit. |

Classify the permission separately from the installation method. Start with ROW occupancy/land use; street opening or excavation; lane closure/traffic control; restoration, inspection and close-out; pole attachment, conduit occupancy or make-ready; easement/license/franchise; and conditional environmental or crossing authorizations. Trenching, directional boring, aerial installation and collocation describe the work. A lane-closure plan may be a condition, not a separate permit. A private pole-owner agreement is not a government permit. Treat small wireless facilities and tower siting as distinct from wireline fiber; verify their own application rules before adding them.

For each source preserve the official title/type, ID type, issuer or decision body, applicant, location(s), owner of the ROW, scope, work description, important conditions, action and dates, source URL, exact locator, retrieval date and archived evidence. Track project, instrument, event and location separately. One instrument spanning several jurisdictions can appear in each local view but counts once nationally. Publish jurisdiction-level counts when a route is not explicitly mapped; do not draw an inferred line between addresses. Distinguish “this register returned no matching record” from “the register wasn't available.”

## 4. Verify the pilot, transfer it, then scale

1. Establish the legacy baseline and protect existing IDs, count definitions, CSVs and shared-session edits. For each pilot register log search terms, date range, pagination, result count, rejected matches and access gaps. Verify matched records against the issuing agency or signed instrument; hold candidates that rest only on a search snippet.
2. Map units separately: rules, distinct documented permits by status, and other actions. Show totals by category in each default tab; hover, keyboard focus and touch should reveal the same local counts. Check that an application, hearing, easement authorization and issued permit never collapse into four issued permits. An unrelated municipal fiber project must not increase data-center totals.
3. Extend the existing COMPASS-style evidence, jurisdiction, status and taxonomy checks. Add independent fixtures for the four Fairfax cards, the VDOT number and dates, the two-stage easement, an unavailable archive and a multi-jurisdiction permit. This tests an adaptation of COMPASS; it is not a claim of NLR certification.
4. Run the repository's full build and browser UAT in the execution session. A user arriving on a filtered list should identify the instrument, deciding office and timing **without expanding a card**, open the government source in one click, and reach the detailed evidence in one disclosure. Test desktop and mobile, keyboard, no clipping/overflow, search by official title and ID, CSV and map parity, local-file loading with network disabled, existing performance/interaction budgets and collector isolation. Publish measured results, not just a pass flag.
5. Repeat the same cases in Ohio and Texas, starting from their busiest data-center jurisdictions under the atlas ranking and checking the actual road/utility owners. Record what transfers unchanged and what needs an authority-specific adapter. Then continue the ranked state/territory queue, including municipalities, townships and overlapping authorities. After local expansion, add federal ROW grants and project-specific federal crossing/environmental decisions from the issuing offices; keep grant funding and consultation milestones distinct from permits.
6. At the end of each two-state batch, leave a coverage ledger, counts by evidence type, data-size growth, measured usage if available, and per-jurisdiction collection/review cost. Use these observations to project the next batch; do not extrapolate from Virginia permit volume alone.
