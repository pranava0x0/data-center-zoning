# Iowa: state air equipment, signed instruments and city actions

The [state construction-permit plant index](https://idnraqrr.iowadnr.gov/ConPermitSearch/ByConstructionPermitPlantNameView) returned 3,284 distinct listed plant IDs. The replay checks every listed plant name for data center/centre and server farm plus three reviewed aliases: Questa [city record](https://www.councilbluffs-ia.gov/DocumentCenter/View/9195) (same DNR plant number as Gable), [CyrusOne's own identification of its Council Bluffs facility](https://www.cyrusone.com/data-centers/north-america/council-bluffs-ia), and Siculus (state-issued Title V document, SIC 7374 and NAICS 518210; construction register itself had zero rows). Eight selected plants yielded 4,626 printed construction rows. Two identical printed rows for 14-A-117-S1 become one observation. The 4,625 resulting equipment permit versions reduce to 1,005 distinct base permit numbers within facilities. These are equipment permits, often for individual standby engines, **not 1,005 data centers**. Some equipment histories are rescinded. A revision without an affirmative current status is unconfirmed. The state warns that pre-2000 records may not be imaged, newer permits are issued frequently, and rendered copies are not operative instruments.

Five separate signed [state operating permits](https://www.iowadnr.gov/environmental-protection/air-quality/operating-permits/draft-final-permits) are validated against permit number, facility number, printed issue and expiration fields. None is declared active solely by its printed expiration. Four [Council Bluffs trade permit](https://www.councilbluffs-ia.gov/2531/Monthly-Permit-Reports) observations represent three city numbers, including two dates under 2400752. [West Des Moines council resolution 26-024](https://www.wdm.iowa.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/44648/639045806609170000) records a major site-plan decision, not an issued building permit. West Des Moines projects span Dallas, Warren **and** Polk counties; we used plant records and the site address for resolution 26-024, not a city-to-one-county assumption.

Cutoff November 15, 2026. Run `python3 scripts/collectors/iowa/history.py` (offline) or add `--refresh` to fetch the DNR register again. Cached compressed HTML carries the row data and document references; signed operating permits are available offline in original form. For city rows, direct access was restricted; the agency's indexed report was read and the observations pinned in `data/history/iowa/local.json`. Their hash covers that manifest, not a city PDF. The municipal scope is three historical Council Bluffs monthly reports and one West Des Moines meeting. Current-year Council Bluffs reports are by request from the city's published archive. This is not statewide municipal coverage and missing plants or rows are not a negative permit finding. Future aliases, city portals, parks under construction, individual-case zoning and sites in local air jurisdictions require follow-up. See `data/history/iowa/validation.json` for event rankings and denominators. By source event frequency: Council Bluffs/Pottawattamie 3,764, West Des Moines 866; ranking partly reflects how the regulator splits equipment and revisions.

State DNR’s [industry-specific explanatory page](https://www.iowadnr.gov/programs-services/business-regulatory-assistance/regulations-data-centers) separates state water, air and stormwater triggers from municipal zoning. [Dubuque Ordinance 37-26](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/dubuqueia/latest/dubuque_ia/0-0-0-59603) provides an adopted time-limited local example with four alternative triggers and specific exceptions. Confirm any extension, repeal or replacement before applying it to a project.
