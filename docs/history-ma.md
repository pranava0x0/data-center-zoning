# Massachusetts

The [Boston ISD approved building permit register](https://data.boston.gov/dataset/approved-building-permits) was queried across description and comments. Project matches include historic enterprise rooms, substantial colocation buildings, electrical/fire-safety work, expansions and decommissions. Search scans the source with a count first, checks pagination and preserves the city permit number. Two amendment records identify the original authorization. Occupancy certificates are explicit decision records rather than new construction permits. The agency defines Open as valid, Closed as expired: where the “Open” row also records a past expiry, the collector declines to call it active. The 147 cases found can neither establish new development nor be equated with 147 sites. [Cambridge Open Data](https://data.cambridgema.gov/) new construction, additions/alterations and short-form permit series were queried in both applicant and approved work description fields; this exact search found no hits. That does not establish no data center permits there or elsewhere in Middlesex County.

In the top mapped county, MassDEP’s [recommended ruling](https://www.mass.gov/doc/markley-group-llc-recommended-final-decision-2025-022/download) identifies July 3, 2025 as the issue date for NE-25-002 at Markley in Lowell; the appeal filing, recommendation and [commissioner affirmance](https://www.mass.gov/doc/markley-group-llc-final-decision-2025-022/download) remain separate stages. The [Secretary’s MEPA certificate](https://eeaonline.eea.state.ma.us/EEA/emepa/mepacerts/2020/sc/npc/15988%20NPC%20Markley%20Data%20Center%202385%20Line%20FINAL.pdf) concerns a **distribution line** supporting the facility in three Middlesex municipalities. Its 2020 project-change notice and conclusion are not building permits; the certificate records a July 11, 2019 state water-quality certification with no individual certificate number. The downloaded original includes the municipality list. Indexed MassDEP documents were read when the direct host returned 403, so those four event hashes cover the extracted fact manifest, not copies of the decision.
