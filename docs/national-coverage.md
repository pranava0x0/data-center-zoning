# Nationwide expansion

[Search the jurisdiction directory](../viewer/coverage.html) or download the [geography table](../data/national/jurisdictions.csv), [local government units](../data/national/government_units.csv), [tribal governments](../data/national/tribal_governments.json) and [tribal areas](../data/national/tribal_areas.csv). A place in these tables is a search target. It does not establish that a particular government issues a permit, has a data center or has passed an ordinance.

* The [2025 Census Gazetteer](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/time-series/geo/gazetteer-files.html) and [Census TIGERweb](https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/tigerwebmain/TIGERweb_main.html) cover 50 states, DC and the five inhabited U.S. territories. Counties include county equivalents. Incorporated municipalities, functioning subdivisions, other subdivisions and statistical places remain distinguishable. A Census place is not necessarily a zoning authority. Census codes are strings: keep leading zeros. The [nine minor outlying areas](../data/national/outlying.json) have separate [Interior Department references](https://www.doi.gov/oia/islands/politicatypes).
* The [2022 Census of Governments](https://www.census.gov/data/datasets/2022/econ/gus/public-use-files.html) lists active general-purpose authorities, special districts, school districts and dependent school systems in separate sheets. Neither a utility name nor a school boundary establishes jurisdiction over a private project. Staff did not contact any agency.
* BIA's [January 2026 Federal Register notice](https://www.federalregister.gov/d/2026-01899) reports 575 recognized entities. Its two lists contain 348 and 229 printed entries. The directory retains all 577 printed names; the discrepancy is not resolved by dropping names. The separate 1,305 tribal land/subdivision entries are Census geography, not sovereign-government counts.
* [National priority](../data/national/priority.csv) ranks the [publisher's October count of operating, under-construction and planned facilities/projects](https://dcmap.us/states/). The 52 published rows cover states, DC and Puerto Rico; the other four inhabited territories have no comparable published count. A blank is not zero. County and city rankings in the state dossiers use PNNL facility markers and municipal boundaries and therefore need not sum to the state directory.
* The [government website pass](../output/national_local_checks.csv) records attempted sources, pages read and possible mentions. A municipality with no dated ordinance or permit is not credited with a positive or negative legal finding. The [source replay](../scripts/ordinances/discovery/national_local_screen.py) runs offline by default; use `--fetch --limit 500` to check the next 500 distinct websites in count order. State and local collections are scoped snapshots. The [separate federal release](federal-permits.md) began after the original local-source queues had been reconciled.

## What the source search reached

The [national gate](../output/national_scope_check.json) counts geographic entries, Census government units and listed websites separately. Census offices with no website, sites that failed retrieval and unvisited source types never count as a finding that the community permits or bans a data center. Special districts were selected for water, wastewater, fire, utilities, power, flooding or planning in their names. Other special districts and school systems remain in the roster; their functions were not assigned by assumption. The national file covers the five inhabited territories with local government systems; the nine minor outlying islands are a separate Interior Department reference, not invented municipal governments.

* [Website results by geography](../output/national_jurisdiction_checks.csv) and [by government office](../output/national_local_checks.csv). A Census-designated place is statistical; it need not issue its own permits. A township and an incorporated town with similar names can be different authorities.
* [Local code and meeting referrals](../data/national/local_code_referrals.csv) link a publisher back to the government page that referred readers there. The directory offers these links where available. A referral does not establish the version in force.
* [Government codebook search](../data/national/codebook_search_status.json), [within-document passages](../data/national/codebook_matches.json), and [reviewed claims](../data/national/codebook_claims.json). Only manually reviewed operative provisions are published, with their source and cited page.
* [Positive-control results](../output/local_search_quality.json): compare the short homepage search with localities where independent research found rules. A keyword match may refer to a different provision; these results do not estimate national recall.
* [Territorial registers and dated decisions](territorial-reviews-astra.md), [tribal government website results](../data/national/tribal_site_checks.json), and [construction stormwater coverage](state-construction-rules.md). Tribal policies concerning trust land or consultation are attributed to the tribal government; neither a state outline nor a reservation boundary extends them to all land inside the outline.

A county subdivision and an incorporated city can share a name. The [geography check](../scripts/pipeline/national_geocheck.py) tests a township's government type before assigning a website and records supplemental attempts separately. The [known-positive comparison](../output/local_search_quality.json) shows how often a limited homepage search missed locations where other government evidence established a rule. These are diagnostic cases selected because they have evidence, not a random sample of the country's jurisdictions.
