# Florida: local permits, water decisions and municipal dockets

The November 16, 2026 snapshot has **31 actions**: 13 numbered permits in the South Florida Water Management District's permit view, ten additional water application actions, one issued City of Miami building permit, one unissued city application, one finalized Miami-Dade County mechanical permit, and five Eatonville council agenda appearances. The independent Palm Beach Project Tango docket is not copied here.

**Selection.** The [PNNL/IM3 mapped centroid inventory](https://immm-sfa.github.io/datacenter-atlas/im3_datacenter_centroids.geojson) (November 2, 2026) has just 17 Florida records, including apparent duplicate points. Miami-Dade and Orange counties lead with four each, Hillsborough has three, Broward and Duval two each. Intersecting with [2026 Census BAS incorporated places](https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/Places_CouSub_ConCity_SubMCD/MapServer/11) puts three in Eatonville, two each in Miami and Jacksonville, five outside incorporated places. The counts are a workflow priority only; they do not establish operational facilities, planned sites or MW.

**Miami, Miami-Dade, Hillsborough and Tampa.** Separately queried [Miami iBuild](https://gis.miami.gov/gis/rest/services/Maps/iBuildPermits/MapServer/0), [Miami-Dade's building register](https://gisweb.miamidade.gov/arcgis/rest/services/MD_LandInformation/MapServer/1), [Tampa permits](https://arcgis.tampagov.net/arcgis/rest/services/Planning/PermitsAll/FeatureServer/0), and [Hillsborough issued residential/commercial permits](https://maps.hillsboroughcounty.org/arcgis1a/rest/services/SDEMapServices/RegulatoryZoning/MapServer/3). Search forms and fields are in the config. Tampa and Hillsborough returned no matches under these predicates, not evidence that no permits exist. County metadata describes the county point layer as [covering the last three years](https://gisweb.miamidade.gov/GISSelfServices/Data/HTML/BuildingPermit.htm), not the full archival register. City of Miami's explicitly named Equinix remodel and elevator application share a folio and operator. Eighty-four additional county-parcel-identifier matches in the **city** layer lack verified data-center scope and are published separately in `data/history/florida/related.json`, excluded from confirmed permit totals. Six county colocation/antenna results are held. County code F is decoded as Finalized using the agency's own field domain. Time of day is converted to Eastern unless the feed uses a midnight UTC date-only value.

**Water records.** The [district permit view](https://geoweb.sfwmd.gov/agsext1/rest/services/Regulation_ApplicationPermits/RegBndSearch_RegPermitting/FeatureServer/2) and [application view](https://geoweb.sfwmd.gov/agsext1/rest/services/Regulation_ApplicationPermits/RegBndSearch_RegPermitting/FeatureServer/0) cover the district, not all Florida. Screened 19 and 31 records respectively by project name. Selected records explicitly describe data centers or a data backup center; “Core Site” alone is not sufficient. Grain is application-number/permit-number pair, deduplicated across views, with the permit view taking precedence. Older project modifications remain actions but do not inflate distinct issued permits. An absent IssueDate remains absent: AppFinalActionDate is explicitly a decision date and not a reconstructed issuance date. Categories include consumptive water use and environmental resource permits; a landscape water authorization is not industrial cooling-water permission. Dates/statuses concern these instruments only. No county inferred from city names or permit prefixes.

**Orange County / Eatonville.** Captured official town [agreement and reimbursement agendas](https://meetings.municode.com/adaHtmlDocument/index?cc=EATNVLLEFL&ip=True&me=eee85cd3aa0f4023bb662a6405cdd735), including the [September 2026 review of amendment 3](https://meetings.municode.com/adaHtmlDocument/index?cc=EATNVLLEFL&ip=True&me=5cc05f3d22c94e3a9921ec730be886ec). An agenda item asking for approval does not establish a vote. A historical Eatonville newsletter describes early-start/civil permits but its former URL is unavailable to this collector. No invented permit ID or date. Jacksonville points remain a gap; the [city states](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/public-works/building-inspection-division/building-permit-and-inspection-utilization-reports) pre-JaxEPICS data require a public request. The Florida DEP air search did not pass our robots preflight; the district water data is a separate official agency source.
