# Second ordinance pass: state powers, local approvals and tribal jurisdiction

As of November 16, 2026. The [combined rule file](../data/national_rules_astra.json) extends the original 19 stable records with 18 additional provisions, grouped by government and version. [The source manifest](../data/national_rules_astra_sources.json) stores raw URLs, content hashes and archive paths for positive sources. Failed-source attempts are tracked separately and do not support published rules. Run `python3 scripts/ordinances/rules/national_rules_expansion.py` to replay this extension and verify every old and new rule anchor; `python3 scripts/ordinances/rules/national_rules.py` verifies the combined file. The Santa Fe clerk's seven-page PDF has no embedded text; the original bytes, page references and hashed OCR are retained together.

| Priority geography and observed-point evidence | Published evidence and limits |
| --- | --- |
| KS: Johnson County 5, led by Lenexa 3 and Olathe 2 | In the same industrial corridor, [De Soto](https://www.desotoks.us/465/Data-Centers) affirmatively identifies M-1 as a permitted-use district while preserving project/site review. [Sedgwick County](https://www.sedgwickcounty.org/communications/news-releases/this-week-in-sedgwick-county-sept-2-2026/) documents its extension through December 24; this is limited to the unincorporated county, not a Wichita adoption. Olathe's online use-matrix PDF was discoverable but did not pass crawler preflight; no unverified district matrix was imported. No countywide Johnson County conclusion follows from the two city searches. |
| KY: Jefferson County 2; Boone and Floyd 1 each | The [Metro Council enacted O-135-26](https://louisville.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?FullText=1&GUID=7405E0CE-574D-407B-AF7B-2D278775C4C6&ID=8172008&Options=&Search=). Provisions distinguish the named planning bodies, grandfathered applications scheduled for hearings, and Metro building review of existing structures. The commission's September permanent-rule recommendations and November committee agenda did not establish their own enactment. Suburban cities have independent authority; the metro records lack an assigned geographic code to avoid overstating jurisdiction. |
| WV: Kanawha and Morgan counties one point each | The [state statute](https://code.wvlegislature.gov/5B-2-21B/) supplies statewide authority for **certified** high-impact centers and microgrid districts. The [90 MW test](https://code.wvlegislature.gov/11-6N-2/) concerns critical IT load plus other requirements. The [filed final rule](https://apps.sos.wv.gov/adlaw/csr/readfile.aspx?DocId=58817&Format=PDF) sets a petition clock. The exemption is not recorded as independent permissive county zoning or as an air, water or federal permit. |
| OK: Tulsa County 8, with 7 Tulsa city points | [Tulsa's code](https://tulsaplanning.org/plans/TulsaZoningCode.pdf) addresses accessory tower height/clearance and floor-use-specific parking. The [November staff packet](https://tulsaplanning.org/pc/agendas/2026-11-04-ws.pdf) independently confirms the 2024 classification and March 2026 pause. Later draft standards are not promoted. Oklahoma City's [November council announcement](https://www.okc.gov/News-articles/Oklahoma-City-Council-extends-moratorium-on-new-data-centers) was readable during government-site research but machine archival preflight returned 403; it is documented as an outstanding primary-document capture, not a published row. |
| NM: Bernalillo County 9, Valencia 5, Doña Ana 3; Albuquerque 7 and Los Lunas 5 | The [Albuquerque IDO](https://documents.cabq.gov/planning/IDO/2025_IDO_Update/IDO_2026_Effective-2026-05-06.pdf) mentions electronic data processing within a broader definition. No campus-level zoning pathway was inferred. Los Lunas funding/LEDA actions were not treated as zoning laws. The lower-ranked Santa Fe County supplied its [actually recorded Ordinance 2026-06](https://www.santafecountynm.gov/documents/ordinances/Ordinance_2026-06.pdf), cross-checked with the [official adoption announcement](https://www.santafecountynm.gov/news/detail/santa-fe-county-approves-18-month-moratorium-on-data-centers): 1 MW or more, 18 months from July 9 recordation, exemptions and vested rights. Sections directing staff to study cooling or noise are not substantive proposed-facility limits. |
| Additional tribal and territorial authority | [Salt River Pima-Maricopa Indian Community's zoning ordinance](https://srpmic-nsn.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/ZONING-ORDINANCE-SRO-467-2015.pdf) sets a conditional-use process requiring landowner consent or authorization. [Saipan's conditional-use article](https://www.cnmizoning.com/resources/files/law/2024%20AMENDED%20SAIPAN%20ZONING%20LAW%20OF%202013/2013%20SZL_Article%207_2013.pdf) establishes designation and approval requirements. Both are general procedures: neither establishes that a data center is a conditional use. Saipan law does not cover Tinian or Rota. Cheyenne and Arapaho Tribes' [own council index](https://www.cheyenneandarapaho-nsn.gov/tribal-council/resolutions) was archived; its linked final-resolution CDN did not pass collection preflight. Seminole's TR 2026-38 and Eastern Band of Cherokee Indians' Ord. 158 were not verified from a captured final tribal instrument. None of these three bans is asserted from a newspaper or the earlier tabled/agenda item. |

Ranking units are mapped facility points, not proven projects or gigawatts. See the state's `data/history/{kansas,kentucky,new_mexico,oklahoma}/rank.json` or `data/history/west_virginia/rankings.csv` for the underlying atlas and boundary vintages.
