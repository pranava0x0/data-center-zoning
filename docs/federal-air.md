# EPA-issued air construction permits for data-center generators

The search found **four final federal actions for two North Dakota generator facilities** on the Fort Berthold Indian Reservation. Three actions are versions of one facility's approval. Both are **40 CFR Part 49 tribal minor New Source Review (NSR) permits issued by US EPA Region 8**. Neither is a tribal-government-issued permit, a North Dakota state permit, a federal PSD major-source permit, a Title V operating permit, proof of an operating site, or a zoning approval. The permits concern generation for small on-site computing facilities using oilfield gas; the evidence does not establish AI/hyperscale or emergency-only operation.

## National coverage and exclusions

[Regional CAA permit directories](https://www.epa.gov/caa-permitting/caa-permitting-your-region) led to actual federal issued and pending registers in all 10 EPA regions. The [screen coverage table](../data/national/federal_air_registers.json) and [raw extracted register rows](../data/national/federal_air_register_rows.json) enumerate the snapshot. Across 33 configured sources (32 successfully archived), 627 table rows and the text outside those tables were screened for computing terms, operator aliases and a separate crypto/mining lead category. These are **source table rows, not 627 unique permits**. Four matched: the two confirmed facilities above, a [Great American Mining application labeled on hold at the applicant’s request](https://www.epa.gov/caa-permitting/caa-permit-actions-pending-epa-region-8) without independent computing-use confirmation in that register, and “Microsoft Word” in signing instructions. The latter two do not enter project permit history. The accessible Region 2 and Region 9 registers were screened for the Caribbean and Pacific territories; no additional site-specific federal air permit was established. Region 4’s broader PSD link redirected to a client-rendered Permit Hub with no table in the archived body. The tribal and historic federally issued GHG pages for that region were independently screened. A negative textual match is not a legal determination that a jurisdiction lacks data-center permits. Facilities may use LLC names, appear only inside attachments, or sit in programs administered by states/localities. The screen did not claim to read every attachment of every nonmatching industrial facility.

## Which federal program to check

1. **Identify the actual issuer and location.** Federal Clean Air Act standards often appear in state permits. Use [EPA’s approval/delegation table](https://www.epa.gov/caa-permitting/psd-air-permit-program-approval-and-delegation), the [Part 71 program description](https://www.epa.gov/title-v-operating-permits/epa-issued-operating-permits) and regional program lists. A permit copied into a federal portal or reviewed by EPA is not necessarily issued by EPA. Federal land ownership does not answer that question.
2. **Screen construction emissions, equipment and geography.** [40 CFR §49.153](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title40-vol1/pdf/CFR-2025-title40-vol1-sec49-153.pdf) provides pollutant-specific minor NSR thresholds, including attainment/nonattainment distinctions. At a source otherwise subject to this program, §49.153(c)(9) excludes qualifying generators designed solely for outage power only under aggregate manufacturer-rated horsepower limits: below 500 hp in nonattainment areas classified serious or lower; below 1,000 hp in attainment areas. These are strict less-than thresholds summed over units, not per-generator allowances. The subsection does not grant a general exemption for data centers or continuous on-site generation. For major construction/modification, [§52.21](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title40-vol3/pdf/CFR-2025-title40-vol3-sec52-21.pdf) sets PSD criteria; the commonly cited 100/250 tons-per-year source thresholds depend on source category and regulated pollutant. Actual permit limits, common-source determination, attainment classification and project changes matter.
3. **Distinguish construction authorization from operation and equipment standards.** [Part 71 §71.3](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title40-vol17/pdf/CFR-2025-title40-vol17-sec71-3.pdf) defines covered stationary sources; Part 71 is the federal operating-permit program. An engine meeting a construction exemption can still be subject to equipment standards. For the applicable stationary compression-ignition emergency-engine category, [§60.4211(f)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title40-vol8/pdf/CFR-2025-title40-vol8-sec60-4211.pdf) distinguishes real emergency use, maintenance/testing and restricted nonemergency use. Its 100-hour annual provision does not authorize unrestricted peak shaving, routine power sales, or prime operation. Equipment model, fuel and manufacture date can change which standards govern.
4. **Check current orders and effective text.** The archived GovInfo sources are the official 2025 codification; use current eCFR and operative amendments for a new project. [EPA’s data-center CAA resource page](https://www.epa.gov/stationary-sources-air-pollution/clean-air-act-resources-data-centers) distinguishes guidance, proposals, final measures and particular emergency orders. An announcement, guidance memorandum, pending application or DOE reliability order is not itself a site-specific federal air permit.

## Reproduce

Run `python3 scripts/collectors/federal/epa_air_cases.py` for an offline replay or `python3 scripts/collectors/federal/epa_air_cases.py --refresh` for a bounded, robots-aware update. The [source configuration](../data/national/federal_air_sources.tsv) includes register, authority-guide, facility and regulatory-document roles. A changed set of register matches requires new explicit reviewer dispositions. `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_federal_air.py` checks chronology, stable identity, effective/issue dates and negative controls. Raw originals and byte/text hashes live in `cache/history/federal-air`; [four normalized action rows](../output/history/federal/epa_air_cases.json) feed the atlas. No upstream index or permit is changed.
