# Minnesota project and permit evidence

The first release finds 38 government-reported events through November 15, 2026: 11 Minneapolis construction/trade permits, 20 Minnesota Environmental Quality Board review records for six projects, and seven dated local votes on Chaska's Cloud HQ proposal. State agency and municipality are recorded separately. A government environmental review may study **an alternative development scenario**, not a committed data center.

The [PNNL/DOE atlas](https://github.com/IMMM-SFA/datacenter-atlas) supplies 17 geolocated markers. Against Census incorporated-place polygons Minneapolis and Chaska tie at four; Hennepin (six), Carver (four), and Dakota (two) lead the counties. See [state-specific rankings including unincorporated points](../data/history/minnesota/rank.json). The 89 directory entries used to pick the state are a different source and definition. More review records were recovered in Dakota County, which does not mean it has more built campuses.

* [City of Minneapolis construction permits](https://services.arcgis.com/afSMGVsC7QlRK1kZ/arcgis/rest/services/CCS_Permits/FeatureServer/0): public work descriptions matched data center / processing center / server farm. Compare ID-only count with all returned object IDs. Identical mapped duplicates are grouped strictly by issued permit number, with metadata required to agree. Exclude two permits converting a former data center into pickleball courts. Remaining records include government data rooms, chillers, interior demolition and additions. The source marks 10 permits Closed with completion dates; BLDG1181293 is Issued, without contemporary proof it remains in force. “CO required” in a description is not an occupancy certificate.
* [EQB environmental review database](https://www.eqb.state.mn.us/environmental-review/environmental-review-data): twelve separate whole-calendar-year searches from 2015 through the cutoff check project title and summary (including scenarios), then fetch project-specific histories. The first nonempty year was 2022; earlier empty responses do not establish historical absence. Individual action/notice dates explicitly distinguish approval for publication from a decision or adoption. EQB sends related projects in one response; accept only exact project IDs, never adjacent docket histories. The [MPCA](https://www.pca.state.mn.us/data-centers) distinguishes environmental review from enforceable permits and does not decide whether construction proceeds.
* [City of Chaska Cloud HQ](https://www.chaskamn.gov/841/Cloud-HQ): seven commission/council concept, AUAR, conditional-use and preliminary-site-plan votes (2022–24). Final site-plan/plat approval still requires a separate application; no final permit is inferred. Unrelated project names and off-site work are not matched solely by nearby addresses.

Run `python3 scripts/collectors/minnesota/history.py` for an offline replay or add `--refresh` to refetch the agency registers. [Query audit](../data/history/minnesota/validation.json) records each yearly query size and city candidate total; the [status mapper](../scripts/collectors/minnesota/status.py) determines what can count as a permit. These sources do not cover every local permit, untagged project, municipal hearing or exemption.

[MPCA’s air roster guidance](https://www.pca.state.mn.us/air-permits-in-minnesota) points to [What’s in My Neighborhood](https://webapp.pca.state.mn.us/wimn/search) and notes it does not contain every earlier air permit. A separate state-site-name query returned 35 candidate **sites**. The public permit-action endpoint returned an access challenge and the CSV activity export was also unavailable; no site was promoted to an air permit. State site “Active” describes activities associated with a site, not an individual permit’s validity. The site response also presented that challenge on repeat; the observed candidate count is a discovery note, outside the repeatable history dataset.
