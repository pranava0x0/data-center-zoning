# New Jersey

The release contains 28 dated records across six state, regional and municipal sources. The source snapshot is bounded: 13 New Jersey State Library issues returned by the full-text search of the [official DEP Bulletin collection](https://dspace.njstatelib.org/server/api/discover/search/objects?query=dc.title%3A%22DEP%20Bulletin%22), from 2024 through the November 15, 2026 cutoff, plus three regional board packets and a municipal agenda. All available original PDF versions in the returned items were screened (2,815 pages, including alternate copies). The search covered data center/centre/datacenter, Equinix, CoreWeave and CoreSite; the Library's search index, issue coverage, OCR, unrecognized aliases and historical start date all limit recall. Distinct milestones are keyed by program, activity/permit number, status and actual event date. Repeated notices and alternate copies are attached to one event.

The [August 2025 bulletin](https://dspace.njstatelib.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/3ac1a971-9028-463b-9849-c198c6082bc2/content#page=99) reports revocation of Carteret NJG0338079; page 196 reports an unrelated issued general authorization for Kenilworth. The [November 2025 issue](https://dspace.njstatelib.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/e483e093-34f0-4a2c-bee9-23feda316b6e/content#page=60) records a QTS wetland-line verification and a separate combined land-use action covering flood hazard, wetlands crossing and transition-area waiver. The verification is not itself construction authority. A combined application is not counted as three distinct permits. The [Secaucus activity](https://dspace.njstatelib.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/aed09044-90ac-4cb3-8680-67753a9247c2/content#page=63) shows review steps before approval; at that same campus, an NJPDES proposed individual stormwater action in October 2026 is not final. A separate treatment-works application identifies Equinix NY03. Four issued NJPDES general coverages retain their real 5G2/5G3 codes; one other numbered coverage was expressly revoked. Current active standing is not verified by a years-old issue date.

[Regional Meadowlands case 24-291](https://njmc.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/pdfs/meeting/2024/sept-agenda.pdf#page=18), [22-235](https://njmc.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/Final_Board_Book2_7_28_22.pdf#page=18) and [22-439](https://njmc.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/pdfs/meeting/2022/dec-agenda.pdf#page=21) document receipt of zoning certificate requests and a dated suitability review. That date is not the final approval date or date of filing. The [Bridgewater Township agenda](https://bridgewaternj.gov/agenda-library/planning-board-meetings/2026-planning-board-meeting-documents/2026-agendas-planning-board/1162-03-17-26-planning-board-meeting-agenda/file) lists a proposed lot for a data center; a short pinned public-reader excerpt is used for this one municipal item because direct download returned 403. It is not an approval.

The [pinned lab inventory](https://github.com/IMMM-SFA/datacenter-atlas/tree/fe4d8420f9fdc7b16b82b8461c5e4c39b93d8f1d) contains 59 New Jersey markers. Hudson 14, Somerset 13 and Middlesex 12 lead. Census places alone miss 22 markers. Joining the [2024 county-subdivision map](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/GENZ2024/shp/cb_2024_34_cousub_500k.zip) resolves every marker to a municipality: Secaucus 11, Piscataway 10 and Franklin Township in Somerset County 8. CDPs are never treated as permitting governments. Franklin Townships in other counties are distinct governments. No direct municipal permit registry was collected for Piscataway or Franklin; this remains a gap, even though their counties are represented. New Jersey's [DataMiner program](https://dep.nj.gov/opra/dep-info/) lists facility-specific air reports but the public endpoint returned a challenge page; no records were fabricated or recovered from third-party copies.

Run `python3 scripts/collectors/new_jersey/history.py` for an offline hash-checked replay and `--refresh` to rediscover the specified bulletin cohort and fetch all its original PDF versions.
