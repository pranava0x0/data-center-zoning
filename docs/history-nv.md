# Nevada: 42 government events in 13 registers and case-file series

The 2026-11-15 reporting cutoff applies to historical events and the interpretation of snapshot status. A dated application, a site, a permit version and an issued instrument are different things here.

The [PNNL physical inventory](https://github.com/IMMM-SFA/datacenter-atlas/tree/fe4d8420f9fdc7b16b82b8461c5e4c39b93d8f1d), pinned at commit fe4d842, supplies marker points and county names. The TX geometry reader joins them to [2024 Census place boundaries](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/GENZ2024/shp/cb_2024_32_place_500k.zip). Incorporated-place leaders tie at two: Henderson, Las Vegas and North Las Vegas. Enterprise CDP has 11; Sunrise Manor four; Paradise two. Sixteen points lie outside mapped Census places, all in Washoe and Storey; “Reno” mailing addresses cannot be substituted for city jurisdiction. This is enumeration of the inventory, not market census, planned MW or statewide permitting prevalence.

The [EPA ICIS-NPDES bulk register](https://echo.epa.gov/tools/data-downloads/icis-npdes-download-summary) contributed seven federal copies of state general permit coverages with status EXP. The exact facility/permit-ID join examined 5,977 facility-interest and 6,843 permit-version rows. All seven have the same EPA issue date; five different effective dates appear, and these are separate columns. Four facility rows have no county code: the output uses statewide jurisdiction and preserves the mailing city as a separate field. Verify current coverage with NDEP.

[Switch's signed county permit](https://www.clarkcountynv.gov/assets/documents/government/departments/environment_and_sustainability/division_of_air_quality/permitting/16304-20250515-per.pdf) records a 2021 issue and 2025 revision in one term. The expiration shown is June 30, 2026; the county separately reports a renewal received December 3, 2025. Neither expiration nor continuation is established for today's operation. Two 2025 dust-permit observations likewise do not establish present status. The eight [county stationary sources](https://maps.clarkcountynv.gov/arcgis/rest/services/AQM/StationarySource_NonEdit/MapServer/1) without issue evidence remain discovery leads in history/nevada/leads.json.

Coverage limits: no exact accepted data center in the [Las Vegas city layer](https://services1.arcgis.com/F1v0ufATbBQScMtY/arcgis/rest/services/OpenData_Building_Permits_/FeatureServer/0); the single [Reno broad match](https://services5.arcgis.com/RjM4BJrv5ZkqP4XC/arcgis/rest/services/Permits_Issued/FeatureServer/0) concerned GPS data and sewer work. The Henderson grading layer contained zero matching narratives; county inspection records independently reference early grading but do not prove the separate grading permit's issue date. North Las Vegas' [city self-service guide](https://eg.cityofnorthlasvegas.com/EnerGov_Prod/selfservice/guide/Citizen%20Self%20Service%20Guide.pdf) documents project searches; the local example here is a **county** air notice at a North Las Vegas address, not a city building permit. Clark's broader citizen-access POST did not deliver a usable public search response; no results were inferred. The [NDEP general permit portal](https://genpermits.ndep.nv.gov/Common/SelectNOI.aspx) directed a search to a sign-in screen. Queries miss aliases, plans lacking narratives and uncatalogued town/city files; no exhaustive issuance counts.

Run `python3 scripts/collectors/nevada/history.py` for an offline build; it verifies archived hashes, reconciles query object IDs, checks page anchors, excludes unrelated keyword hits, and regenerates rankings, output and validation counts. `--refresh` replaces city GIS searches, case documents, the lab inventory, and state registers. A fresh [national EPA ZIP](https://echo.epa.gov/files/echodownloads/npdes_downloads.zip) may be provided via `--epa-zip PATH`; otherwise that explicitly pinned source remains unchanged. Review changes to document locators and status vocabularies before accepting a replacement snapshot. Nevada reuses TX filtering/geography and the EPA state-slice routine; local field/date mappings, project aliases, PDF page anchors and agency status semantics require explicit configuration. The searchable UAT anchors include 16304 (signed county permit vs renewal), BCOM2018024998 (cancelled before issue), and WAC23-0012 (application vs subsequent decision).
