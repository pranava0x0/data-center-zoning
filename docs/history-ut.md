# Utah project permits

The [national hotspot file](../data/national/hotspots.csv) ranks Salt Lake County first with 13 mapped facility markers, Utah County second with six and Washington County third with two; Bluffdale leads places with five. Facility markers and the events below have different units. A state-reported municipality is not parcel-level proof of city zoning jurisdiction.

The [Division of Air Quality public portal](https://daqpermitting.utah.gov/) provides separate forms for current notice-of-intent (NOI) applications, [issued approval orders](https://daqpermitting.utah.gov/AOsIssuedAll), [draft orders](https://daqpermitting.utah.gov/AOsOutForCmt), and operating-permit/EPA-comment reports. We executed the agency form queries and retained their returned row sets, counted in [validation](../data/history/utah/validation.json). Of 20 possible NSR approvals, 19 are verified data-center-related orders, including equipment modifications and dedicated generation; one gas dehydration plant order had an obsolete or conflicting site name and no demonstrated causal link. The scope covers state-tagged sites, not all equipment anywhere in the state. The Web page advertises issued orders since July 2008; 931 returned rows and their histories do not establish a complete archive of superseded orders.

The 45 recovered events comprise 19 issued orders, 17 earlier application-receipt dates actually printed on those covers, six current application observations, one draft/public comment start, and two city events. Each approval uses the document's printed DAQE number. The [scanned QTS order](https://daqpermitting.utah.gov/DocViewer?IntDocID=155764&contentType=application%2Fpdf) is pinned by SHA and visually verified. NOI listings use the state's completedDate field, which is not a permit issue date. Approval order issue or amendment does not establish continuation, site operation, or land-use approval. Two named centers may have separate generating equipment or more than one regulatory site registration.

[Bluffdale’s December 6, 2023 minutes](https://www.bluffdale.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Minutes/_12062023-1316) record the 5–0 site-plan vote and conditions (engineering, state access permit, sewer district, plats and municipal boundaries for Phase 2, fire review). The [Salt Lake City GIS](https://maps.slc.gov/server/rest/services/Accela/Accela_Permits_v2/MapServer) active engineering, building, transportation and planning layers produced one direct data-center demolition application, BLD2024-05216. It is not an issued construction permit or a new facility. Legacy state Socrata links and the city’s session-based historical interface did not yield a reproducible historic permit feed in this run. No statewide local coverage inference follows.

[Utah Code §73-5-8.3](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title73/Chapter5/C73-5-S8.3_2026050620260506.pdf) and [Eagle Mountain’s QTS agreement](https://eaglemountain.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/QTS-Development-Agreement.pdf) are recorded separately under [applicable rules](../data/rules_ut.json). The QTS agreement describes a site-specific excavation/site-plan review sequence. A later Bluffdale moratorium was on the council agenda; without an executed ordinance/minutes it is not represented as enacted here.

`python3 scripts/collectors/utah/history.py` rebuilds offline. `python3 scripts/collectors/utah/history.py --refresh` queries agency tables, city layers and authority-hosted documents. New or changed unreadable approval identifiers fail closed. No EPA ICIS rows are copied.
