# Tennessee permit history

The sample contains **50 actions**, of which 44 are dated (December 2009–October 2026). Six Metro Nashville files have no issue date. There are 28 Metro Codes files, 19 versions of 11 state water permit numbers in EPA ICIS, two Franklin Planning Commission approvals, and one Shelby County air construction permit. Multiple versions and trades are not buildings. Coverage cutoff: November 16, 2026.

**Where to look.** The [PNNL IM3 atlas](https://im3.pnnl.gov/visualizations) lists 24 Tennessee [mapped centroids](https://immm-sfa.github.io/datacenter-atlas/im3_datacenter_centroids.geojson) (v2026.11.02). The county order is Williamson 6; Davidson and Montgomery 4 each; Sumner 3; Shelby 2; Grainger, Hamilton, Henry, Roane and Rutherford 1 each. Intersecting those records against the [2026 Census BAS incorporated-place polygons](https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/Places_CouSub_ConCity_SubMCD/MapServer/11) gives Nashville-Davidson metropolitan government (balance) 4, Brentwood/Franklin/Gallatin 3 each, Memphis 2, Chattanooga/Oak Ridge/Smyrna 1 each, and 6 outside. Google buildings near Clarksville and three buildings at Meta Gallatin illustrate why this is not a count of independent campuses, projects, operators or capacity. County fields on EPA rows come from the ICIS county code if reported; three historical versions lack a verified county; none is guessed. The source's postal locality is retained separately.

**What the records establish.**

- **Williamson County.** The [Franklin Municipal Planning Commission](https://webapi.legistar.com/v1/franklintn/matters) voted to approve the [Peak 10 site plan](https://franklintn.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=2394&GUID=160E4FA7-8378-48BA-BBB3-92A4B304779E) in 2015 and the [Eddy Lane site-plan revision](https://franklintn.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=3824&GUID=A67DD281-837E-447D-8813-9EB1F9075771) in 2016. Both have a recorded passed action; neither is a building permit. The latter also corroborates TN water file TNR241376 by project and address. No Brentwood permit is inferred from three mapped records. The [city operator portfolio](https://www.flexential.com/data-centers/tn/nashville) identifies facilities for subsequent local searches.
- **Nashville/Davidson.** The municipal [Parcel Viewer](https://maps.nashville.gov/ParcelViewer/) publishes a [Codes Permit History field definition](https://maps.nashville.gov/ParcelViewer/scripts/config.js). The collector resolves four mapped Davidson points, specified known address patterns, and an explicit federal-court parcel through city ownership GIS, deduplicates the nine APNs and screens all 564 returned records. Require an explicit data center, centre, ctr, data hall or server farm in the work description. Phones and contractor fields are not needed for the output. Examples: [Ezell Road parcel](https://maps.nashville.gov/parcelservice/permithistory/14810013500) includes numbered generator work; [Grassmere parcel](https://maps.nashville.gov/parcelservice/permithistory/13300013500) includes historical Asurion work and later DC BLOX files marked HOLD/OPEN. The [court opinion, p. 2](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-tnmd-3_26-cv-01143/pdf/USCOURTS-tnmd-3_26-cv-01143-0.pdf) explicitly identifies that parcel; case allegations are not used as permit evidence. A city issued date and a current snapshot status are distinct. DONE or EXPIRED with no issue date is insufficient to infer issuance. OPEN/HOLD does not imply a January 1 application date from the permit number.
- **Montgomery/Clarksville, Sumner/Gallatin and Shelby/Memphis.** [EPA’s ICIS-NPDES bulk downloads](https://echo.epa.gov/tools/data-downloads/icis-npdes-download-summary) provide facility IDs, permit names, dated permit versions, raw status, and term dates. The statewide scan covers 29,810 facility rows and 52,555 permit rows; links identify specific [Clarksville](https://echo.epa.gov/detailed-facility-report?fid=TNR241940), [Gallatin](https://echo.epa.gov/detailed-facility-report?fid=TN0082279), and [Memphis](https://echo.epa.gov/detailed-facility-report?fid=TNR155608) files. Case rows with an explicit facility/permit name are supplemented by carefully matched named infrastructure. The [Memphis council packet](https://memphistn.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/xAi-Additional-Documents.pdf) describes the Colossus water-recycling plant as serving multiple users; force-main/plant permit versions are ancillary works, not other data centers. Unconfirmed alias-only hits such as industrial-park files are retained as leads, outside the counts. An ECHO search that matches an entire facility group does not prove every member serves the same project.
- **Memphis air.** [Permit 01156-01PC, signed p. 1](https://web.archive.org/web/20250703012450id_/https://www.shelbytnhealth.com/DocumentCenter/View/7174/01156-01PC-070225Permit) authorizes construction of 15 natural-gas turbines at CTC Property LLC’s Colossus site. Issue July 2, 2025; printed expiration January 2, 2027. It is not an operating permit. The [county’s original URL](https://www.shelbytnhealth.com/DocumentCenter/View/7174/01156-01PC-070225Permit) returned 404 at collection. A Wayback capture of the original official URL and an [independently hosted unannotated copy](https://rachelandthecity.com/the-xai-files/records/county-and-epa/shelby-county-air-permit-01156-01pc-2025-07-02.pdf) returned byte-identical signed scans. Page 1 was visually verified and OCR-checked against the pinned hash.

**Limits and repeatability.** Nashville’s [citywide open-data listings](https://www.nashville.gov/departments/codes/codes-administration/public-records-and-data/daily-reports) and [Shelby Develop901 project locator](https://www.develop901.com/projectlocator) identified registers, but their hosted GIS robots checks returned HTTP 403 or 504. Franklin's hosted historical GIS similarly failed preflight. The active collector preserves these gaps; it neither treats the probes as archived cases nor assumes a zero. The former Nashville Socrata endpoint redirects to a retired service. The independent city parcel register, EPA compliance data, Franklin governing-body votes, and archived county instrument are the primary sources actually counted. Follow-up priorities: Brentwood construction/occupancy files; county-issued building files at the two Memphis campuses; local grading and zoning in Clarksville/Montgomery and Gallatin; parcel predecessor histories in Nashville. State EPA water coverage is not statewide building coverage.

Run `python3 scripts/collectors/tennessee/history.py` from the project root for offline reconstruction; `--fetch` resumes configured public queries. Source hashes refer to uncompressed API/PDF bytes except where ICIS rows cite the national ZIP, whose hash, member CRCs and Tennessee slice hashes are also pinned. To reproduce state slices from an identical national ZIP use `history_ms_la_epa.split(path, ('TN',))`. The config keeps query predicates; the manifest keeps the executed URLs; the coverage audit reports separately any newly reachable GIS candidates awaiting review. No blocked host is needed for offline assembly.
