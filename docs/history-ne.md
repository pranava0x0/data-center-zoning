# Nebraska

The [Nebraska DWEE public record search](https://dwee.nebraska.gov/about/public-records-searches) documents what is in the archive, a 500-result ceiling, and the need for a facility number and program. We reproduced its public AIR query at the IDs verified by [CLOP’s notice](https://dwee.nebraska.gov/news-events/public-notices/clop-omaha-ne-llc), [Fireball’s notice](https://dwee.nebraska.gov/news-events/public-notices/fireball-group-llc-0), [Raven’s notice](https://dwee.nebraska.gov/news-events/public-notices/raven-northbrook-llc-0), and the [state’s inventory roster listing TierPoint](https://dwee.nebraska.gov/sites/default/files/publications/2025%20Emission%20Inventory%20Request%20List_0.pdf). The complete four query results total 760 files. The collector fails on a truncated search and retains dated applications, draft permits, public notices, actual issued permits and notifications of construction start; it omits invoices, general correspondence, fee receipts and unclassified documents. It does not duplicate the national NPDES records.

Numbered issuance is verified by the signed PDF, including manual transcription of older scans pinned to SHA-256. One file indexed “DEQ Issued Permit” is the consultant's request concerning engine certificates and confidentiality; the document controls and this is not a permit. The agency hosts duplicate document references or response letters that lead back to the same issuance. Identical facility, event type, program ID, date, description and permit number are combined. The state issues new printed numbers even for amendments; we retain those instrument numbers and identify eleven wholly superseded instruments from replacement covers. A reference revising a single condition does not mark the entire original historical. Remaining validity is unconfirmed. “Document Date” is not labeled a signed issue date in ECM: the earlier Fireball instrument, for example, has a later revision referring to an issue date one day after the index date. Property location for the four older Raven instruments is Springfield as printed on their covers; other Raven workflow activities use the agency notice locality of Papillion. No exact municipality is inferred from location coordinates.

All six overlapping layers of the [Lincoln commercial construction register](https://gis.lincoln.ne.gov/public/rest/services/Planning/Commercial_New_Construction_Permits/MapServer) are searched using project/work terms and deduplicated by city permit number. Four distinct permit numbers survived. A [county health department draft](https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/files/sharedassets/public/v/1/health-dept/environmental/air-public-notices/pubnot20260204a2.pdf) separately documents prior issuance of air permit 229; it does not establish issuance of the proposed modification. Two [Papillion](https://www.papillion.org/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/ArchivedAgenda/_08052025-480?packet=true) [administrator reports](https://www.papillion.org/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Item/3582?fileID=50985) establish footing and plumbing issuance, both unnumbered in the source and excluded from unique permit counts. The main building record is coded revisions required despite a date in a column called Issued Date. City rows are limited to a reviewed indexed report manifest; its hash is not the original city PDF hash. Actual [Sarpy zoning text](https://www.sarpy.gov/237/Regulations) distinguishes special use in BG from permitted principal uses in BGH and IL, not a universal rule for incorporated municipalities.

Run `python3 scripts/collectors/nebraska/history.py` for frozen replay; `--refresh` fetches official facility indexes, original permit PDFs and municipal GIS results. An unfamiliar scanned PDF is held out of permit metrics pending review. County ranking through November 15, 2026: Sarpy 127 events, Lancaster 5. The first priority nationally mapped location was Sarpy County; other major targets, especially Omaha/Douglas County, remain incomplete. Facilities under undiscovered aliases, all other cities, records outside the four selected IDs and city register scopes are unknown, not zero.
