# Data center permit history

[Search the records](../viewer/history.html) · [Federal actions](../viewer/federal.html) · [National directory](../viewer/coverage.html) · [All rows (CSV)](../output/permit_history.csv) · [Counts by register and date coverage](../output/permit_history_summary.json)

The file combines published **permit files, agency actions, applications and case events**. One campus can have many of each. A status is what its source reported; the date is the type of date shown alongside it. For example, an application received in 2023 can now say approved without furnishing an approval date. Blank dates stay blank. The coverage is a documented search of public records, not a census of every Virginia permit.

The current national release is assembled from [registered source series](../data/history/series.csv). [Counts by state and source](../output/permit_history_summary.json) report both date range and the number of rows with dates. More than one dated event can belong to the same permit; equipment-heavy projects can account for hundreds of rows. [Federal program notes](federal-permits.md) explain which events are direct federal actions. [National search coverage](national-coverage.md) identifies where public files have not yielded project records.

The ranking unit is an observed inventory point, potentially one of several buildings at the same campus. It is neither installed MW nor a count of permit files. Virginia's own [existing-building accessibility](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title13/agency5/chapter63/section432.5/) and [construction/demolition permit](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title13/agency5/chapter63/section80/) provisions are separately sourced. They do not import Texas’s registration system. See the [COMPASS crosswalk](compass-crosswalk.md).

County selections include records labeled for that county plus records whose agency supplies that site county. When a city crosses county lines, its permits remain city-only unless there is explicit county evidence. A city’s published zoning rules never automatically become county rules.

## Virginia examples

- **Belmont Innovation Campus, Loudoun, LEGI-2023-0048.** The [county Planning Commission report](https://loudoun.granicus.com/MetaViewer.php?clip_id=7704&meta_id=248008&view_id=89) records denial on March 13, 2024, and reconsideration on March 19. The [Board’s release](https://www.loudoun.gov/m/newsflash/Archive/Item/9060?arcId=16806) confirms approval of the smaller 1.3-million-square-foot version on April 10. These are three events on the same case. The first two dates were checked in the indexed government report; the county release is archived locally.
- **IAD 165, Fairfax, FSPRK-SHL-2023-01546.** The [Fire Marshal's record](https://plus.fairfaxcounty.gov/CitizenAccess/Cap/CapDetail.aspx?Module=Fire&TabName=Fire&agencyCode=FFX&capID1=REC23&capID2=00000&capID3=02T4X) lists a sprinkler/standpipe permit issued February 13, 2024. It is a distinct approval from the building shell or rezoning.
- **Google at Greenfield, Botetourt.** The [county’s project timeline](https://www.botetourtva.gov/1021/Google-Data-Center) places receipt of the initial grading applications in February 2026 and issuance of the land-disturbance permit in August. Only the months are supplied. Grading approval did not authorize impacts to wetlands.
- **Project Loch, Chesterfield.** The [state workflow](https://permits.virginia.gov/Permit/Details/129165) records an individual wetlands application. A separate surface-water determination or a public notice is not a final wetland permit.

## Rebuild or adapt

Run `make history` in the project folder to rebuild the history from the local snapshots. It makes no model calls. The build validates record IDs, dates against the edition cutoff, required fields and source URLs. The source collectors perform their own hash, schema or row-count checks. Each source JSON preserves fields specific to that agency; the combined CSV keeps those fields under `details`. The smaller viewer export omits them.

| Source pattern | Reusable collector | What to change |
|---|---|---|
| ArcGIS permit and plan layers | [history_loudoun.py](../scripts/collectors/virginia/loudoun_county/building_and_zoning_gis.py), [configuration](../data/history/virginia/loudoun_county/building_and_zoning_gis/config.json) | Supply layer URLs, identifiers, date roles, timezone, terms and file paths. Confirm what “issued,” “submitted” and parcel rows mean locally. |
| County monthly building reports and classified structures | [history_loudoun_supplement.py](../scripts/collectors/virginia/loudoun_county/monthly_reports.py), [report and layer settings](../data/history/virginia/loudoun_county/monthly_reports/config.json) | Reuse the two PDF table patterns and deduplicate by agency file number. Check the page headings and printed date ranges for the local format. Compare structures by parcel and date only as candidate links. |
| Published government CSV or CKAN catalog | [history_catalog_csv.py](../scripts/collectors/multistate/ckan_catalog_csv.py), [configuration](../data/history/multistate/ckan_catalog_csv/config.json) | Point to the approved CSV or a CKAN JSON catalog with a single CSV resource. Map columns and choose the date role. `--config`, `--output` and distinct slugs isolate new runs. |
| Agency HTML table | [history_air.py](../scripts/collectors/virginia/statewide/deq_air_permits.py), [configuration](../data/history/virginia/statewide/deq_air_permits/config.json) | Map table headers, registration identifiers and date semantics. Preserve editions and removals. If the government site denies access, use an authorized export or withhold the finding. |
| Environmental GIS and application tracker | [history_water.py](../scripts/collectors/virginia/statewide/deq_water_permits.py) | Reuse export capture, paging, matching and reconciliation; map the new regulator's program fields and action codes. Do not carry Virginia’s legal meanings into another state without checking them. |
| Accela and board decisions | [history_cases.py](../scripts/collectors/multistate/accela_case_pages.py), [configuration](../data/history/multistate/accela_case_pages/config.json) | Seed official detail URLs or narrowly scoped case-event anchors. Preserve distinct date precision, board actions and technical permit types. Takes `--config`, `--cache` and `--output`. |
| Other state/local government directories | [jurisdiction_sweep.py](../scripts/ordinances/discovery/jurisdiction_sweep.py) | Give it a verified CSV with `jurisdiction,url,geoid` and `--name your-region` to run the same bounded discovery used for Virginia. Candidate pages do not become verified permits automatically. |

Refresh against new source material as a **new edition**; keep the former source manifests. Export completeness applies only to the named source, term screen and vintage. Data centers listed under shell-building descriptions, owner aliases or unrelated project names can be missed, and towns can maintain independent files. Complete local trade, fire, water-service and inspection histories outside the collected jurisdictions require additional official registers or agency records requests. No records requests were sent.

The [edition summary](../output/permit_history_summary.json) measures raw archive, CSV, state viewer payloads and on-demand details separately. It projects CSV and state payload size for 100,000 similarly distributed records; PDF and GIS archive growth depends on how the agencies publish their registers. See the separate [ordinance research budget](scale.md) for model review assumptions.

[Fairfax site decisions](../scripts/collectors/virginia/fairfax_county/land_use_cases.py) and [Henrico monthly building reports](../scripts/collectors/virginia/henrico_county/permits.py) illustrate why a “final” agency record needs a date role: approval of a floodplain study is different from issuance of a building permit. [Water and fire cases](water-fire-audit.md) require evidence that the regulated activity belongs to the data center rather than a former or neighboring user. [Central Ohio's hearing records](metropriority.md) retain a denied site plan, its later approval, and distinct approvals for a construction-support plant.

Choose **Case history** on a card to see other dated actions on the same file without leaving the results. [Grouping](../scripts/pipeline/case_timelines.py) uses a matching agency case or permit identifier in its geographic scope. Common operators, similar facility names and adjacent parcels do not by themselves join histories.

[Elk Grove Village](elk-grove-permits.md) illustrates why the parcel address and a contractor's mailing address are stored separately. [Independent Manassas city](manassas-city-history.md) reports dated permit and occupancy actions without issuing record numbers in its development update. They remain findable as history even when a distinct permit cannot be counted. [The archive receipts](evidence-receipts.md) cover every published history record.

Run `make replay-sample` to compare the output of 15 collectors across 24 series against the current edition. The [cross-jurisdiction replay](../output/replay_transfer_sample.json) reproduced 3,802 records without a byte change, including the original Virginia, Ohio, Texas, Arizona and Indiana adapters. This verifies repeatability against the archived inputs; a live refresh can still uncover portal or rule changes.
