# Maryland

Starting from the top mapped local hotspot, we queried the [Baltimore housing department’s 2019–present permit register](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=189e6d1c65df4e13b38c0027cee574f6). Its canonical service moved from “egisdata” to the “baltegis” URL declared in the city's ArcGIS item. Search work descriptions, project names and previous/proposed use. Four of 12 matched cases are changes to an identified original city permit. The city labels these via IsPermitModification and cites the underlying case in each description; both date roles and the original instrument are preserved. Work through what was the *former* data center is explicitly labeled as consolidation/decommissioning. Baltimore is an independent city, not Baltimore County.

Eight selected original [MDE case documents](https://mde.maryland.gov/datacenters/Pages/FrederickDataCenter.aspx) establish 28 dated events. Two premises-wide signed air permits cover arrays of generators; the cover’s control number is used to identify the instrument rather than treat every registered generator as a permit. Three signed water-use instruments authorize dewatering for site or utility construction. The date inside the permit controls, distinct from the later/earlier transmittal date and first historical appropriation. No volume is treated as cooling consumption. The three land restoration events include a March 2023 conditional plan approval and its explicit May withdrawal, and approval of the separate Bauxite 2 temporary utility EMP. A November 9 regulatory letter addresses 20 individual NOIs, including an authorization, seven continuing between programs, and twelve pending. Every event generated from the letter is an agency notice, with no new permit issuance inferred. Do not join it to a federal source using just the general permit, date or project name. Site place entries follow the issuer (Adamstown, Frederick, or county when only the project is identifiable); they do not establish whether the City of Frederick has land-use jurisdiction.

Run `python3 scripts/collectors/maryland/history.py` from local cache; add `--refresh` to refresh Baltimore and the eight state files. Raw hashes bind the visually reviewed permit covers and groundwater/surface-water parameters in `data/history/maryland/review.json`; changed documents are held rather than silently reusing old findings. Coverage is limited to one city series and the selected documents in a single case collection, not all MDE or Frederick records. As of November 15, 2026: Frederick County 28, Baltimore city 12 events. Montgomery County and any federal or interstate datasets are separate. County’s [adopted zoning map](https://www.frederickcountymd.gov/DocumentCenter/View/359197/Adopted-Critical-Digital-Infrastructure-Comprehensive-Plan-Amendment) and [planning report](https://www.frederickcountymd.gov/ArchiveCenter/ViewFile/Item/16893) distinguish the overlay from base industrial zoning.
