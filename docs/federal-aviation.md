# FAA airspace studies

The [FAA's obstruction evaluation program](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/obstruction_evaluation) looks at the effect of a proposed structure or crane on navigable airspace. A determination of “No Hazard” does not authorize construction, zoning or operation. A building, its corners, and temporary construction equipment can have different study numbers. The atlas shows these as dated actions; they contribute **zero** to the map's permit totals.

The [official region and year catalog](https://oeaaa.faa.gov/oeaaa/oe3a/external/portal-api/getReportYearsPerRegion.do) listed 401 off-airport files spanning 1960–2026 at collection. Of these, 400 contained a recognizable table; one was empty or unreadable. The parser counted 1,807,354 rows beginning with a study number, of which 31 did not have the expected columns. It found 1,863 explicit data center or server-farm descriptions or structure names. It published 1,679 distinct completed determinations across 34 states and held 184 matches, including nearby facilities, different structure uses, and cases without a qualifying dated outcome. This is a text search, not a census of all aviation reviews associated with a campus.

For example, [the signed FAA letter for 2019-AWP-15211-OE](https://efiling.energy.ca.gov/GetDocument.aspx?DocumentContentId=63885&tn=232020), released in a California Energy Commission docket, identifies a data center building and a February 3, 2020 determination. That study number is missing from the published bulk case set. The letter supplies the decision directly; the atlas preserves its separate provenance. Other cards link to the regional file and supply the exact study number. The reported city is not used to assign local zoning authority. Where the state/county code and the agency's county name disagree, the record stays at state level.

Run `python3 scripts/collectors/federal/faa_obstruction_evaluations.py --fetch --from-year 1960` to check the catalog and obtain the off-airport archives; omit `--fetch` for offline replay. [File hashes, counts, held matches and errors](../data/national/federal_aviation_search.json) are preserved separately from the published cards.

The [signed-letter supplement](federal-aviation-letters.md) adds 30 no-hazard findings and eight extensions from four California projects. Seven study numbers also appear in the bulk results; none has the same decision and date there. This is why a study number, a dated agency action, a building and a permit must not share a single count.
