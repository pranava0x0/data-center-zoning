**Signed environmental decision rejected.** The [viNGN FONSI](https://www2.ntia.doc.gov/sites/default/files/grantee/4816_virgin_islands_fonsi_040411.pdf) is an actual NTIA environmental determination signed April 4, 2011 (PDF p. 14). Its scope (pp. 1–2) is middle-mile fiber and small enclosed fiber access points. The [environmental executive summary](https://www2.ntia.gov/sites/default/files/grantee/4816_virgin_islands_executive_summary.pdf), PDF p. 11, confirms that scope. Neither network enclosures nor anchor institutions are evidence of a data-center land-use action. Separately, the [VIHFA signed 2022 services contract](https://cdbgdr.vihfa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/3.-Business-Technology-Group-Contract-No.-2022-CDBG-DR-006-DC-SIGNED.pdf) includes data-center disaster-recovery functions. It procures technology services, not a new facility; not treated as another grant.
