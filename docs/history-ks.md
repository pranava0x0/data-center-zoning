# Kansas project records

The archive contains nine City of De Soto land-development and meeting actions, seven with an exact day. These are decisions, contracts and agendas, not issued building permits. The [city's Beale project history](https://www.desotoks.us/496/Beale-Data-Center) records Phase 1 conditional site-plan approval; its August 2025 reference to approval of the development agreement is distinct from the September 23 as-of date on the executed agreement. The city describes the October 1 [supplement addressing water](https://www.desotoks.us/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/357). The [October 27 planning minutes](https://www.desotoks.us/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Minutes/_10272026-689) table two Phase 2 site plans and separately approve and forward a plat subject to easement acceptance. The [Digital Realty history](https://www.desotoks.us/498/Digital-Realty-Data-Center) lists meetings; an agenda alone is not a vote.

The pinned PNNL data-center point inventory and Census incorporated-place boundaries place five of seven Kansas observations in Johnson County: three Lenexa and two Olathe. Hays (Ellis County) and Medicine Lodge (Barber County) have one each. Points are neither permit IDs nor a current capacity estimate. Explicit data-center text returned no IDs in the Olathe permit layer; two operator-term matches did not verify data-center work. The Overland Park permit layer describes a rolling window of the current and two preceding years, but its query returned 403. Edgerton could not be archived through the collector preflight. No jurisdiction with missing results should be assigned zero permits.

Replay with `python3 scripts/collectors/kansas/history.py`. The config records exact phrases/pages, the manifest pins response hashes, and coverage records source failures. For future follow-up request historical permits and dated case dispositions in Lenexa, Olathe, Overland Park and Edgerton.
