# Federal permits and project decisions

Open **Permit history → Federal only** in the [atlas](../viewer/index.html#view=permits&issuer=federal). A history card shows what happened, which agency acted and when. An agency’s public notice, consultation or environmental review can tell you why a project was delayed without being a permit to build. [Download the federal records](../output/federal_history.csv) or [inspect the reconciliation](../output/federal_quality.json). Records stop on November 15, 2026; more recent changes may exist.

| Project activity | What to check | What the atlas counts |
|---|---|---|
| Clear, excavate or grade land; runoff will leave the site | Disturbing at least one acre, including work on a smaller parcel in a larger common plan, can trigger construction stormwater coverage. [EPA construction program](https://www.epa.gov/npdes/stormwater-discharges-construction-activities) and [state authorizations](https://www.epa.gov/npdes/npdes-state-program-authority). | Only an identified EPA-issued permit is federal. State-issued permits may appear on the same EPA website. Each permit number counts once; a termination is a history event. |
| Discharge dredged or fill material into federally regulated waters or wetlands | Check applicability with the district and verify any individual or general permit. [EPA overview of Clean Water Act §404](https://www.epa.gov/cwa-404/permit-program-under-cwa-section-404). | The [Corps search](federal-corps.md) separates issued individual permits and verified general permits from notices and jurisdictional determinations. A district file can group several actions. |
| Build on or cross federal public land | Determine who manages the land and whether the proposed use needs a [BLM right-of-way](https://www.blm.gov/programs/lands-and-realty/rights-way). | Nevada case NVNV106360814 includes the [BLM decision](https://eplanning.blm.gov/Project-Home/?id=f4dd06fc-446f-f111-ab0d-001dd802f839) and the [administrative stay](https://www.bcnv.org/DocumentCenter/View/17364/2026-0213-Boulder-City-Stay-Granted). It does not count as active. |
| Install or modify combustion equipment on lands where EPA implements the air program | Check the applicable source category, aggregation rules and the permitting authority. The [federal air review](federal-air.md) links to regional registers and explains the limited scope of the engine exemptions. | The North Dakota records include two EPA tribal-air permit families with original and revised instruments. The latest issuance alone does not establish current status. |
| Construct a tall structure or use cranes near an airport | FAA [obstruction evaluation](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/obstruction_evaluation) can apply to structures and temporary equipment. | The [FAA study register](federal-aviation.md) retains completed findings. Several may cover different corners of one building. None count as a permit to build. |
| Seek a federal approval or federal project decision | A federal agency may have to evaluate environmental effects, consult other agencies or document an applicable exclusion. [NEPA review process](https://www.epa.gov/nepa/national-environmental-policy-act-review-process). | The [FAST-41 screen](federal-dashboard.md) retains actual completed steps, omits forecast dates and separates timetable changes from substantive approvals. DOE exclusions are recorded as decisions, not site permits. |
| Develop or study hydropower to support a project | Verify the underlying instrument. An application accepted for filing is not an issued preliminary permit or an operating license. [FERC hydropower licensing](https://www.ferc.gov/industries-data/hydropower/licensing). | The Alaska history records three steps in one preliminary-permit proceeding. No license or permit was inferred. |

[FERC’s Susquehanna order](https://www.ferc.gov/sites/default/files/2024-11/20241101-3061_ER24-2172-000.pdf) rejected a request to raise the co-located power allocation from 300 to 480 MW. The [rehearing history](https://www.ferc.gov/enforcement-legal/legal/court-cases/susquehanna-nuclear-llc-v-ferc) is listed separately. These concern a grid agreement, not permission to build the Pennsylvania campus.

The [DOE case review](federal-doe-sites.md) distinguishes a signed environmental decision from a lease announcement. The [territory award review](territory-grant-review.md) documents two grants for equipment or maintenance; neither identifies a site or grants permission to build.

The [expanded EPA search](../data/national/federal_water_lead_summary.json) also checked operator names, industry codes and alternate facility terms. The [candidate ledger](../data/national/federal_water_leads.csv) explains why a warehouse, a no-exposure certification or a separate solar project cannot establish a data center permit.

The geographic search took place before this federal inventory. [The local-search gate](../output/national_scope_check.json) reconciles the directory and its outcomes; it does not certify that every local record was found. Federal sources also have gaps: exact-name searches can miss a project filed under a subsidiary, operators can own unrelated facilities, non-public cases are absent, and a missing expiration date is not evidence that a permit is active. Direct federal records appear both in this subset and in the full atlas; do not add those totals together.
