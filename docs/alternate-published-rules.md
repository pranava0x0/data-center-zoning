# Rules recovered from alternative names for data centers

The local text screen returned 49 of 1,970 readable government-referred PDFs. This bounded review supports **15 provisions in six additional governments**. “Not promoted” means this review did not establish a rule, not that the jurisdiction has none. Texas, Georgia and Virginia were outside this follow-up. The legal snapshot is November 15, 2026. An edition or publication date is not assumed to be the effective date of a specific provision.

| Government and source | What the text supports |
|---|---|
| [Randolph County, NC, Unified Development Ordinance](https://www.randolphcountync.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1786/The-Unified-Development-Ordinance) | §621, pp. 207–208 names HI-CD, regulates setbacks for outdoor equipment, requires an engineer’s cooling certification, and limits generator testing to four hours per month. The server-hosting definition is on p. 22. The amendment ledger on pp. 475–476 and [commissioners’ August 3 meeting recap](https://www.randolphcountync.gov/m/newsflash/home/detail/2598) confirm the changes. |
| [Brown County, SD, Title 4](https://www.brown.sd.us/sites/default/files/2026-08/title_4_zoning.pdf) | Chapter 4.38, also listed on the [county code index](https://www.brown.sd.us/ordinance/title-4-zoning), specifies separate conditional-use and building permits, a one-mile sensitive-use setback with a limited reduction, and electric-provider documentation for grid-connected operations. The reduction is not automatic. |
| [Saluda, NC, zoning ordinance](https://www.cityofsaludanc.com/images/2025-12-8_Zoning_Ordinance_1.pdf) | The December 8, 2025 restatement includes the December 9, 2024 amendment. C-3’s specific requirements govern permit, plan, acoustics and utility review. Page 87 expressly defines remote storage, processing or distribution. |
| [Mancelona, MI, zoning ordinance](https://villageofmancelona.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Mancelona-ZO-FINAL-12.27.24.pdf) | The [village ordinance page](https://villageofmancelona.org/zoning-ordinances/) identifies the December 27, 2024 edition. Pages 65 and 69 list data processing facilities by right in Commercial and Light Industrial & Warehousing. Four spaces per 1,000 square feet is **maximum** parking. The digital-records/mainframe definition expressly excludes general offices. No claim is made about other districts or permits. |
| [Pitt County, NC, Ordinance 14](https://www.pittcountync.gov/DocumentCenter/View/806/Zoning-Ordinance-PDF) | Page 160 sets separation, height and submittal standards for the large-scale use defined on p. 218. Page 53’s matrix marks RA/GC/HC “CD,” but LI/GI “D” (permitted with development standards); p. 160 uses conditional-zoning language for all five. **No by-right or conditional district decision was published** from that conflict. P. 262 records the 2021–22 changes. The [October 31 public notice](https://www.pittcountync.gov/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/2063) schedules a **proposed** moratorium hearing for Nov. 16, after the cutoff; it establishes no enacted pause. |
| [Springdale, SC, zoning ordinance](https://springdalesc.com/files/18561298369.pdf) | Page 113, paragraph U, includes electronic data storage centers within “Office” and defines the operation of computer hardware separately from a high-technology employee office. This supports one **classification**, not blanket permission. The cover states updated September 2, 2025 while page footers read April 6, 2026; the same category appears in the [March 2024 municipal edition](https://springdalesc.com/files/96905823851.pdf). No new amendment or effective date is inferred. |

**Other leads and holds.** Many historical ordinances use “computer center” alongside typing, software development, training, retail, financial or repair services. A phrase alone cannot establish hosting, intensity, equipment siting or district permission. New Franklin, Ohio and Grundy County, Iowa have blank adoption fields. Hand County’s March draft states a June 2 adoption date, while the [official notice](https://handcountysd.gov/commission/legal-notices/) says July 21 with August 28 effectiveness; the [county index](https://handcountysd.gov/commission/ordinances/) separately labels the draft and adopted copy. We do not attribute that draft’s provisions to the enacted instrument. Marion Township, Michigan’s traditional-use section warrants review alongside its [hyperscale moratorium and amendments](https://www.mariontownship.com/meetings/township-board). The Monona County index alone does not separate digital currency mining from broader hosting. Other unadjudicated candidates remain in the review ledger.

**Offline replay.** Run `python3 scripts/ordinances/codebooks/alternate_publishers.py` with PyMuPDF installed from the project root. It checks original SHA-256, issuer GEOID and source receipts, verifies edition/definition/provision anchors on their own physical pages, and checks the Mancelona and Pitt table columns by PDF coordinates. Every record must pass normalized-text evidence checking. Outputs are `data/national/alternate_published_rules.json`, the 49-document disposition ledger `data/national/alternate_published_review.json`, and `cache/alternate_published_rules/reviewed_index.json` for the ordinary source store. This release does not infer legal absence or publish any project permit.
