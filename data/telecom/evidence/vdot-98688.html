Application Description

APB will place 1771 (1.5) LF of horizontal bore conduit and fiber optic cable along Bolyn Road and bore under the roadway three (3) times. 3 bore diameter will be used throughout the permit. Funding Source: VATI & ARPA Required Deadline: 10/31/2025 DHCD Application #: VATISF#2022-024 / PER ACCEPTED SKETCH - MOT is per VA WAPM. Call VDOT TOC at 703-877-3449 at start and end of daily operations. All requests for temporary lane closures must be in LCAMS and VaTraffic. Work hours: 9:30am-3pm, Monday-Thursday, and Fridays 9:30am-2pm. PLEASE REQUEST FINAL INSPECTION WHEN THE WORK IS COMPLETED.
