What’s counted?

Each ordinance count is a rule, not a whole law. A permit number counts once. Applications, votes and enforcement notices stay in Permit history; they are not permits. Active means the issuing agency reports it is in effect; historical means its record shows it ended. An issue date alone does not tell us whether a permit is still active.

Gray means no matching record in this atlas. County counts include city permits only when the issuing source identifies the county.

Which federal permits apply?

Moving fill into federally regulated waters can require an Army Corps permit. For construction runoff, the issuing agency depends on the location. A federal environmental review or public notice is a separate step.
